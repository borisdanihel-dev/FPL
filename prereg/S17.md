## Design (pinned here; F-H's open choices are marked "pinned")

- **Season:** the 2025/26 replay. These are S6b's inputs: vaastav 2025-26,
  S11's priors from 2024/25, football-data 2025/26, and outcomes re-scored
  under the 2026/27 table by Step 10.
- **Managers:** 200 leagues of 10, sampled at GW1 (GW1 prices, 100.0) by
  S7's sampler with a style weight on `selected`. The style weights are
  pinned:
  - template-heavy: weight ∝ selected (S7's sampler, unchanged);
  - mixed: ∝ selected^0.5;
  - differential: ∝ 1/selected (players with selected > 0).
  - Each league has 4 template, 3 mixed and 3 differential (pinned).
  - The seeds are fixed: league i's managers use seeds derived from 7 and
    i.
  - The squads are fixed all season: no transfers and no chips (pinned;
    stated as a limitation).
- **The truth (the replayed season):**
  - Each week u = 2..38, each manager's XI and captain come from S6b's
    point-in-time xp_v2(u) (from rounds < u), with `rule_autosubs` on the
    re-scored minutes and the captain doubled (S6b's `realised`).
  - GW1 has no forecast and counts for no one (pinned).
  - The final standings are the totals over GW2–38. The winner is the
    highest total; a tie for first shares the win equally.
- **The simulator at origin T ∈ {20, 26, 32}:**
  - The current totals are the truth over GW2..T−1.
  - For each week u = T..38, each manager's XI and captain come from the
    origin's xp_v2 matrix: week T on S5's lambdas (odds, else the
    fallback), weeks after T on the fallback fitted on results before T
    (point 5).
  - Each player-week's points are drawn from its Step 14 PMF at the origin
    (`points_pmf.player_pmf` on that week's xp_v2 detail; the sub PMFs and
    the bonus table from rounds < T).
  - One draw per player-week per simulation is shared by every manager who
    owns the player (shared players correlated). The captain's draw counts
    twice.
  - There are no autosubs inside the simulation (pinned; stated): the PMF
    already carries the 0 of a non-appearance.
  - 2,000 simulations per origin (pinned for the test; Monte Carlo SE of
    P(win) ≤ 0.011), seed 20261018.
- **P(win):** the share of simulations in which a manager's final total is
  the league's highest (ties split).
- **Primary metric (one):** Brier skill = 1 − B_sim / B_leader, pooled over
  200 leagues × 3 origins × 10 managers.
  - B is the mean of (P(win) − won)².
  - The leader baseline: P = 1 / (number tied for first on the current
    totals) for each of those tied, 0 for the rest.
- **Pass:** Brier skill ≥ 0.05, plus the decile calibration table (mean
  P(win) against the observed win rate per decile of P(win)).
- **Information:**
  - the skill per origin;
  - a bootstrap CI over leagues (B = 10,000, seed 20261018, 90%);
  - the lambda sources.
- **Live (information only):** league 455565 from the newest export.
  - The current totals are the standings.
  - Each entry's latest picks are its squad.
  - XI and captain come from the live xp_v2 matrix (the Odds API snapshot
    where it prices the fixture, else the fallback); the PMFs are Step 14's
    live.
  - 100,000 joint simulations (Step 17).
  - Chips (pinned): chips already played are in the totals. A remaining
    Triple Captain or Bench Boost is played in the manager's best projected
    week (the highest captain xP, the highest bench xP). Free Hit and
    Wildcard are not modelled: their squads are unknown.
  - Output: P(win) per entry; dP(win) for our next-week captain over our
    XI's top three by xp_v2, and for the builder's top three single
    transfers (one free transfer, within the bank); common random numbers.
