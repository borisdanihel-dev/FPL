RULING Step 12 (minutes model v2):
1. States start / sub / none, from the player's own recent history before the deadline. GK lock: the keeper with the most starts in his club's last 3 matches gets P(start) = 0.95 unless flagged. covering_for: when a regular starter (60%+ of starts in his club's last 4) is out (live: status not a; replay: 0 minutes in the previous match after starting the 4 before), his most frequent same-position replacement inherits the start share; define it in the prereg, point-in-time only. Congestion factor from days of rest between league matches, fitted on training rounds; dropped if its 90% CI includes 1.
2. overrides.csv on master (player, gw, p_start, reason, timestamp_utc), read by the live mode only; every applied override is logged, and scored later (Brier of override v model).
3. Suspensions as in BUILD_PLAN Step 12 (4 cautions before the club's 19th match, 9 before its 32nd): P(ban next fixture) = P(booking this match) from the per-90 booking rate; it feeds the next fixture's P(start).
4. Test on 2025/26 v the current minutes model: Brier of P(start) better by >= 0.01 with the 90% CI lower bound > 0; MAE of bottomup xP using each P(start) not worse by more than 0.02; goalkeeper-starter mismatches (team-gameweeks whose top-P keeper did not start) <= the current model's. All three must hold.
