- **Item:** S12 (BUILD_PLAN.md Step 12, item N1; offline part).
- **Hypothesis:** a minutes model with start / sub / none states, a keeper
  lock, cover for absent regulars, a fitted congestion factor and
  suspensions predicts starts better than the current model (`starts_last4`
  over the window, scaled by `chance_next_round`) without worsening
  bottomup's points error or the keeper-starter mismatches.
- **Data and sample filter:** 2025/26 through the S4 harness (`canonical_at`,
  clubs of the origin, training rounds < t; the raw rows for yellow cards,
  kickoff times and per-fixture minutes), rolling origins GW5–38;
  population = the recording gate (45+ minutes in the last four), the same
  for both models; 2026/27 live from the newest export and `fpl.sqlite`
  (`player_gw.yellow`, `fixtures.kickoff_time`). Every input is from before
  the deadline (replay: rounds < t; the fixture pairings and kickoff times of
  t known in advance). Definitions pinned here:
  - *States:* P(start) = starts / matches over the club's last min(4, t−1)
    matches before the deadline (the player's own history); P(sub) =
    appearances without a start / the same; P(none) = the rest; live, each
    scaled by `chance_next_round` as the current model is.
  - *GK lock:* in each club the keeper with the most starts in the club's
    last 3 matches gets P(start) = 0.95 unless flagged (live: status not
    `a`; replay: no flags exist); the keeper with the next most starts gets
    0.05, every other keeper of the club 0. A tie for most starts: the
    keeper who started the latest match.
  - *covering_for:* a regular starter = started ≥ 3 of the club's last 4
    matches (60%+). He is out when, live, his status is not `a`; in the
    replay, when he played 0 minutes in the club's previous match after
    starting the 4 before it. His replacement = the same-position teammate
    who started most often in the club's matches of the training window in
    which the regular did not start (ties: more starts overall, then the
    lower id); the replacement's P(start) becomes max(his own, the
    regular's P(start) computed before the absence). No replacement when
    no teammate qualifies.
  - *Congestion:* days of rest = days between the club's previous league
    kickoff and this one (league matches only — cup matches are not in
    the data, stated). Factor c = (start rate of regular starters with ≤ 3
    days' rest) / (start rate with > 3 days' rest), fitted on the training
    rounds at every origin; applied as P(start) × c for players of a club
    with ≤ 3 days' rest, capped at 1; dropped (c = 1) when its 90%
    bootstrap interval over training rounds includes 1.
  - *Suspensions:* cautions = yellow cards this season before the deadline.
    Deterministic: a 5th caution before the club's 19th league match, or a
    10th before its 32nd, in the previous match → P(start) = 0 for the next
    league match. Probabilistic (horizon forecasts, t+1): a player on 4
    (before the 19th) or 9 (before the 32nd): P(ban next fixture) =
    P(booking this match) = min(1, rate90 × P(start)), rate90 = cautions
    per 90 minutes this season (shrunk toward the position mean by the
    same credibility as minutes: m/(m + 4)); P(start)_{t+1} × (1 − P(ban)).
  - *overrides.csv* (live only, on master): columns player, gw, p_start,
    reason, timestamp_utc; applied overrides are appended to
    `tools/out/minutes_overrides_log.csv` (never the projection log) and
    scored once the gameweek settles (`--score-overrides`: Brier of the
    override against the model's P(start) on the realised start).
- **Comparison:** minutes v2 against the current minutes model
  (`fpl_edge.start_probability` on the same inputs, i.e. the rebuilt
  `p_start` of the S4 harness), paired per gameweek on the same players.
- **Primary metric (one):** Brier score of P(start) against `starts ≥ 1`,
  per gameweek, current minus v2 (positive = improvement).
- **Secondary metrics (information only):** the two co-conditions below,
  the share of players touched by each component (lock, cover, congestion,
  suspension), the fitted c per origin with its interval, log loss.
- **Bootstrap:** unit = gameweek, 10,000 resamples, 90% CI (percentile,
  the S4 machinery), seed 20261018.
- **Pass threshold (all three must hold, ruling 4):** (a) mean Brier
  improvement ≥ 0.01 with the 90% CI lower bound > 0; (b) the mean
  per-gameweek MAE of bottomup's xP computed with v2's P(start) minus the
  MAE with the current P(start) ≤ 0.02 (both through
  `project_reliability` with the P(start) swapped in); (c) keeper-starter
  mismatches (team-gameweeks whose top-P keeper did not start) with v2 ≤
  the current model's, summed over GW5–38. Adoption needs Boris's

- **Acceptance tests (in addition):** hand cases for the states, the lock

## Ruling (verbatim)

RULING Step 12 (minutes model v2):
1. States start / sub / none, from the player's own recent history before the deadline. GK lock: the keeper with the most starts in his club's last 3 matches gets P(start) = 0.95 unless flagged. covering_for: when a regular starter (60%+ of starts in his club's last 4) is out (live: status not a; replay: 0 minutes in the previous match after starting the 4 before), his most frequent same-position replacement inherits the start share; define it in the prereg, point-in-time only. Congestion factor from days of rest between league matches, fitted on training rounds; dropped if its 90% CI includes 1.
2. overrides.csv on master (player, gw, p_start, reason, timestamp_utc), read by the live mode only; every applied override is logged, and scored later (Brier of override v model).
3. Suspensions as in BUILD_PLAN Step 12 (4 cautions before the club's 19th match, 9 before its 32nd): P(ban next fixture) = P(booking this match) from the per-90 booking rate; it feeds the next fixture's P(start).
4. Test on 2025/26 v the current minutes model: Brier of P(start) better by >= 0.01 with the 90% CI lower bound > 0; MAE of bottomup xP using each P(start) not worse by more than 0.02; goalkeeper-starter mismatches (team-gameweeks whose top-P keeper did not start) <= the current model's. All three must hold.
END RULINGS (Step 12 part)
