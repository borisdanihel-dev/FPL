- **Item:** S10 (BUILD_PLAN.md Step 10, item H1), lane A, offline under
  `tools/`. It covers 2025/26 only for now (RULING morning 19 Oct, point 3)
  and excludes the hist_pit re-measure, which waits for S4 on master (night
  ruling, item 2).
- **Hypothesis:** the claim under test is that FPL's 2026/27 points table
  (`game_config.scoring`) equals 2025/26's in every points category; only BPS
  changed. If so, scoring 2025/26 with it reproduces FPL's official
  `total_points`. A failure is a finding (Q1), never a reason to change the
  table.
- **Data and sample filter** (every input frozen before any scoring code;
  sizes and SHA-256 in `worklog/downloads_2026-10-19.md`):
  - `historical/2025-26/merged_gw.csv`: local since 17 Oct. Repeated (element,
    fixture) rows are removed by `fpl_hist.dedup_rows` (10 rows, as
    CHANGELOG 2026-09-14 records).
  - `historical/2025-26/players_raw.csv`: vaastav @ `9779cdb`, fetched 2026-10-19T06:24:06Z. It maps each 2025/26 element id to its `code` and
    `element_type`.
  - `historical/2026-27/game_config.json`: the live FPL `bootstrap-static`
    `game_config`, fetched 2026-10-19T06:17:43Z.
  - `historical/2026-27/elements_positions.csv`: the same fetch; maps each
    `code` to its 2026/27 `element_type`.
  - The unit is the player-gameweek: the per-fixture rows of one player and
    one round, summed (a double gameweek is one unit).
  - **Primary population:** player-gameweeks with minutes > 0, i.e. players
    who appeared. Players who never featured score 0 trivially and would
    inflate the share.
- **The points table**, frozen here from `game_config.scoring` (GKP / DEF /
  MID / FWD):

  | Category | Points |
  |---|---|
  | `short_play` (1-59 minutes) | 1 |
  | `long_play` (60+ minutes) | 2 |
  | `goals_scored` | 10 / 6 / 5 / 4 |
  | `assists` | 3 |
  | `clean_sheets` (needs 60+ minutes) | 4 / 4 / 1 / 0 |
  | `goals_conceded`, per 2 conceded | -1 / -1 / 0 / 0 |
  | `saves`, per 3 saves | 1 |
  | `penalties_saved` | 5 |
  | `penalties_missed` | -2 |
  | `own_goals` | -2 |
  | `yellow_cards` | -1 |
  | `red_cards` | -3 |
  | `defensive_contribution`, once a match at the threshold | 0 / 2 / 2 / 2 |
  | `bonus` | 1 per official bonus point (2025/26 BPS) |

  `game_config` gives no thresholds. They come from FPL's scoring rules as
  the project already encodes them (`fpl_edge.py` `DEFCON_THRESHOLD`):
  - 60 minutes;
  - 3 saves a point;
  - 2 goals conceded a point;
  - defensive contribution:
    - DEF: 10 or more CBIT (clearances_blocks_interceptions + tackles);
    - MID and FWD: 12 or more CBIRT (+ recoveries);
    - GKP: none.

  The reproduction test checks these thresholds together with the table.
- **Comparison:** re-scored against official `total_points`, each
  player-gameweek scored under the 2025/26 positions (`merged_gw.csv`
  `position`).
  - The official `defensive_contribution` column is tested against the
    thresholds.
  - Bonus is official.
- **Primary metric (one):** the share of primary-population player-gameweeks
  whose re-scored points are within ±1 of the official total.
- **Secondary metrics (information only):**
  - the same share over all player-gameweeks;
  - the exact-match share;
  - the share by position;
  - each miss attributed to its components (the official minus the re-scored
    per category, where the data allows);
  - DEFCON rebuilt from its components against the official
    `defensive_contribution`: agreement by position;
  - position changes 2025/26 -> 2026/27 by `code`: how many players, how many
    player-gameweeks, and the point change when re-scored;
  - the assists line (Q4) and the BPS line (Q3).
- **Bootstrap:** unit = gameweek, 10,000 resamples, 90% CI, seed 20261019.
- **Pass threshold:** the primary share >= 0.99 (BUILD_PLAN.md Step 10). The
  90% CI is reported; a lower bound under 0.99 is noted, not failed.
- **What the normaliser produces** (`tools/rules.py`): 2025/26 player-gameweeks
  re-scored under the 2026/27 rules.
  - Positions: each player's 2026/27 position by `code`. A player not in
    2026/27 keeps 2025/26's, flagged.
  - DEFCON: rebuilt from its components under that position's definition
    and threshold.
  - Bonus: official, flagged "2025/26 BPS" (Q3: no BPS re-score).
  - Assists: rescaled only if `game_config` shows a 2026/27 assist-points
    change; otherwise the line "assists: no change, no rescale" is printed
    (Q4).
  - Points per category, the total, and the flags.
  - Only 2025/26 rows are used. Nothing here reads a gameweek after its
    own, and nothing feeds a live forecast.
- **Acceptance tests (in addition to the metric):**
  - `tests/test_S10.py`, hermetic, on toy rows:
    - every category and threshold edge: 59/60 minutes, 2/3 saves, 1/2
      goals conceded, 9/10 and 11/12 DEFCON;
    - a double gameweek summed;
    - a missing `game_config` key raises, never defaults;
    - a reclassified player re-scored in the new position;
    - a player absent in 2026/27 flagged.
  - One mutation per rule, each red.
  - The suite green bare, runner-like, with stand-in zones and hermetically.
  - The runner imports nothing under `tools/` (the existing test).

No re-tuning after viewing results.
