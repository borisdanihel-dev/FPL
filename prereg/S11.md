RULING Step 11 (share-of-team allocation with priors):
1. Player xG = lambda_team (S5's odds lambda) x shrunk share of team xG x expected minutes share; xA the same with team xA share. Shares from the player's matches before the deadline.
2. Credibility Z = m/(m + k), m = minutes / 90 this season, k = n(1 - r_n)/r_n from split-half reliability, per metric, measured on 2024/25. Prior = the player's 2024/25 share at the same club; a club change, or no 2024/25 minutes, takes the position mean share at the new club. Finishing skill (G - xG) is shrunk to 0 for the forecast. Manager changes: only if a dated source already exists in the repo; otherwise print "not modelled" (no new downloads without Boris).
3. Test, walk-forward 2025/26 GW5-38, among predicted starters (P(start) >= 0.8): primary family = per-GW Spearman of predicted v realised goals, and of predicted v realised assists, each paired against the current-season-only share model; bootstrap over gameweeks (B = 10,000, seed fixed); Holm across the two at 90%. Each metric is adopted separately only if its Holm-adjusted lower bound > 0. Secondary: log loss of P(goal) = 1 - exp(-xG).
