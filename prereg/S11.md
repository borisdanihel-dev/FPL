- **Definitions pinned:**
  - *Team xG of a fixture* = Σ `expected_goals` of the team's rows in that
    fixture; xA likewise with `expected_assists`.
  - *Current share* s = Σ own xG / Σ team xG over the fixtures the player
    appeared in (any minutes) in the training rounds; 0 with no appearance.
  - *Prior* s0 = the player's 2024/25 share at the same club (over his
    2024/25 fixtures at that club, the whole season) when he played a
    minute for it in 2024/25; a club change or no 2024/25 minutes → the
    mean 2024/25 share of his position's players at his 2025/26 club
    (180+ minutes there); a club without 2024/25 rows → the mean over all
    2024/25 clubs for his position (stated as such).
  - *Credibility* Z = m / (m + k), m = minutes / 90 in the training rounds;
    k = n (1 − r_n) / r_n per metric, measured once on 2024/25: r_n = the
    tie-corrected Spearman of the share in odd against even gameweeks among
    players with 45+ minutes in each half (`measure_reliability`'s
    population), n = those players' mean minutes / 90 per half; printed
    with r_n's 95% interval. Shrunk share = Z s + (1 − Z) s0.
  - *Expected minutes share* = P(start) × m_start / 90, P(start) the
    recording model's (`reliability_inputs`), m_start = the player's mean
    minutes in his starts in the training rounds, else his position's mean.
  - *Forecast* xG_player(t) = Σ over the team's round-t fixtures of
    λ_team(fixture) × shrunk share × minutes share; xA the same with the xA
    shares. Goals forecast = xG, assists forecast = xA (finishing skill
    G − xG shrunk to 0). Manager changes: no dated source exists in the
    repo, so the output prints "manager changes: not modelled".
  - *Comparator* ("the current-season-only share model") = the same
    forecast with Z = 1 (the unshrunk current share, no prior).
- **Comparison:** v2 against the comparator, paired per gameweek on the
  same predicted starters.
- **Primary metric family (two):** per-gameweek tie-corrected Spearman
  (`fpl_edge._spearman`) of forecast against realised goals, and of
  forecast against realised assists; each as v2 − comparator (positive =

- **Secondary metrics (information only):** log loss of P(goal) =
  1 − exp(−xG) against "scored at least one goal", both models; the pooled

- **Bootstrap:** unit = gameweek, 10,000 resamples, 90% CI (percentile,

- **Pass threshold (ruling 3):** Holm across the two metrics at 90%: the

RULING Step 11 (share-of-team allocation with priors):
1. Player xG = lambda_team (S5's odds lambda) x shrunk share of team xG x expected minutes share; xA the same with team xA share. Shares from the player's matches before the deadline.
2. Credibility Z = m/(m + k), m = minutes / 90 this season, k = n(1 - r_n)/r_n from split-half reliability, per metric, measured on 2024/25. Prior = the player's 2024/25 share at the same club; a club change, or no 2024/25 minutes, takes the position mean share at the new club. Finishing skill (G - xG) is shrunk to 0 for the forecast. Manager changes: only if a dated source already exists in the repo; otherwise print "not modelled" (no new downloads without Boris).
3. Test, walk-forward 2025/26 GW5-38, among predicted starters (P(start) >= 0.8): primary family = per-GW Spearman of predicted v realised goals, and of predicted v realised assists, each paired against the current-season-only share model; bootstrap over gameweeks (B = 10,000, seed fixed); Holm across the two at 90%. Each metric is adopted separately only if its Holm-adjusted lower bound > 0. Secondary: log loss of P(goal) = 1 - exp(-xG).
END RULINGS (Step 11 part)
