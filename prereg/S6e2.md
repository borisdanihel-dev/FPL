## Template

- **Item:** S6e2 (RULING 19 Oct evening, point 2; follows F-E / S6e).
- **Hypothesis:** for goalkeepers, xp_v2 as tested plus the expected save
  points picks the starting keeper better than xp_v2 alone.
- **Model:** for goalkeepers, xp_v2 + save points = xp_v2 as tested +
  P(start) × Σ over the round's fixtures f of E[floor(S_f / 3)], where
  S_f ~ Poisson(μ_f).
  - μ_f is S6e's E[saves | start] for that one fixture: (Z × his saves per
    90 + (1 − Z) × the keepers' minutes-weighted mean saves per 90) ×
    (minutes per start / 90) × (λ_opp,f / the round's mean λ over both
    sides of every fixture). Z = m / (m + k), with S6e's functions
    unchanged.
  - Per fixture, because FPL pays per match: a double gameweek sums two
    floors.
  - E[floor(S/3)] = Σ_{j≥1} P(S ≥ 3j), summed until the Poisson tail is
    below 1e-15.
  - k: S6e's split-half rule on the keepers of the season before the test
    season, 2023/24. S6e's code takes k from the prior folder, as S6e did
    for 2025/26 with 2024/25. Pinned here: it is not 2024/25's own
    split-half.
- **Data and sample filter:**
  - 2024/25 GW5–38, S6f's harness (`tools/xp_v2_2425.py`): vaastav 2024-25,
    S11's priors and the saves k from 2023/24, football-data 2024/25 with
    the Ipswich alias for the construction only;
  - outcomes: 2024/25 re-scored under the 2026/27 table by S10b (keepers'
    saves points included; bonus official; DEFCON excluded and refused as
    in S6f);
  - every input point-in-time (rounds < t);
  - starters = forecast P(start) >= 0.8.
- **Comparison:** on S7's 50 squads (seed 7, 2024/25 GW1 ownership), the
  starting keeper chosen by xp_v2 against by xp_v2 + save points.
  - The realised (re-scored) keeper points are paired.
  - If the chosen keeper did not play, the other keeper's points count;
    else 0.
  - This is S6e's `test_2526` loop, run unchanged with the forecasts above.
- **Primary metric (one):** per gameweek, the mean over squads of keeper
  points (xp_v2 + save points − xp_v2).
- **Secondary metrics (information only):**
  - MAE of keeper points among predicted starting keepers;
  - the share of squad-gameweeks where the choice differs;
  - the mean of E[floor(S/3)] against E[S]/3 (the size of the correction).
- **Bootstrap:** unit = gameweek, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261018, 90% CI.
- **Pass threshold:** the 90% lower bound of the primary > 0.
- **On PASS:** adoption at the GW10 checkpoint at the earliest, with Boris.
  2025/26 is never used: S6e saw it.
