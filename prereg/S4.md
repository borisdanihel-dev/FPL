  - *2026/27:* `projection_log.csv` read through the S2 reader rule — for each

    forecast for gameweek g uses only rows with `round < g` plus g's fixture

    A's and has not landed); every 2025/26 figure is printed per position,
    and DEF/GK figures carry the note that 2025/26 BPS rules are superseded.
  - *Sources that qualify for 2025/26 (ruling 2), and why:*
    - `bottomup` — qualifies. `project_reliability()` on `reliability_inputs(
      canonical_through(c, g-1))`, the rolling-origin rebuild already frozen
      in `fpl_hist.py --backtest` (CHANGELOG 2026-09-14): every input is a

      stood before each deadline; `fixtures.csv` carries one undated FDR per
      fixture (FPL revises FDR during a season), and `chance_next_round` and
      `status` are absent. Not rebuildable, so not approximated.
    - `xg` — excluded. `project_xg()` needs FDR (its clean-sheet table is by
      FDR) and `chance_next_round`, both unavailable pre-deadline for 2025/26.
    - `fpl_ep` — excluded. It was not recorded in 2025/26; the `xP` column in
      `merged_gw.csv` has no documented fetch time, so it cannot be shown to
      be pre-deadline.
  - *2026/27 population:* per (event, source), the players in that snapshot;
    a comparison of two sources uses the players both forecast (paired). A
    player forecast but absent from `player_gw` for that event scores 0

    event from `picks` (entry 3073318). 2025/26 — the legal 15 maximising

    DEF, 5 MID, 3 FWD, at most 3 per club) within £100.0m at that gameweek's
    `value`, solved exactly (the item N knapsack / branch-and-bound with
    `selected` as the metric). The squad is a fixed context, the same for
    every source; the models choose only the XI and captain.
- **Comparison:** every pair of sources recorded for the same gameweeks:
  2026/27 own / xg / bottomup / minutes (and `fpl_ep` once W1 records it);
  2025/26 bottomup / minutes. Per-gameweek difference, then the paired
  bootstrap. The report table (Step 4 item 8) prints "not recorded" or "not
  rebuildable" where a cell has no source.
- **Primary metric (one):** n/a — descriptive item (no pass/fail on a model).
  Metrics computed per gameweek, each as Step 4 item 3:
  - tie-corrected Spearman (`fpl_edge._spearman`) and Kendall tau-b, on
    `all` and on `starters`;
  - top-k regret, k = 1, 3, 11: (sum of realised points of the true top k)
    minus (sum of realised points of the source's top k); ties in the
    forecast broken by ascending player_id;
  - MAE of predicted against realised points;
  - Brier score and log loss (probabilities clipped to [1e-6, 1-1e-6]) for
    each binary component a source itself outputs: P(start) (`minutes`;
    rebuilt `p_start`), P(clean sheet) and P(DEFCON >= threshold) (rebuilt
    `bottomup` on 2025/26, where its components are exposed). Outcomes:
    start = `starts >= 1`; clean sheet = `clean_sheets >= 1` with 60+ minutes
    in that fixture; goal = `goals >= 1`; DEFCON = `defensive_contribution >=`
    10 (DEF) / 12 (MID); bonus = `bonus >= 1`. The 2026/27 log holds one
    `predicted` per row, so no 2026/27 source except `minutes` exposes a
    component; P(goal) and P(bonus >= 1) are exposed by no current source.
    Such cells print "no source exposes this component" — never a derived
    stand-in.
  - decision metric: from the fixed squad, the source's best legal XI
    (`fpl_edge._best_xi`, formations 1 / 3-5 / 2-5 / 1-3) by its forecast,
    captain = highest forecast in that XI, doubled; realised points of the
    XI, no automatic substitutions and no vice-captain transfer (a non-player
    scores 0); a squad member without a forecast in the snapshot counts as
    forecast 0.
- **Secondary metrics (information only):** the per-position breakdown of
  every metric; the count of players dropped between snapshots
  (`dropped_players`); the share of the 15 without a forecast.
- **Bootstrap:** unit = gameweek, 10,000 resamples, 90% CI (percentile),
  paired: resample gameweeks with replacement, recompute the mean of the
  per-gameweek difference; seed 20261018. Bootstrap p-value for the Holm and
  Benjamini–Hochberg (q = 0.10) helpers: two-sided, p = 2 × min(share of
  resample means <= 0, share >= 0), floored at 1/(B+1), capped at 1.
- **Pass threshold:** n/a for the sources (descriptive). The item's own bars
  are the self-test bars under acceptance tests. The decision rule every
  later prereg uses is "90% CI lower bound above the stated margin"; the
  power calculator therefore uses alpha = 0.10 two-sided (z = 1.645, the same
  z as one-sided 0.05) and beta = 0.20 (z = 0.842): G = ((1.645 + 0.842) ×
  s_d / Δ)^2. s_d = the standard deviation over gameweeks of the per-gameweek
  difference, measured on 2025/26 for bottomup minus minutes (starters
  Spearman; also printed for `all` Spearman and for the decision metric),
  GW5–38. Printed for Δ = 0.01, 0.02, 0.03.
- **Acceptance tests (descriptive item):**
  1. Self-test, 400 replications, seed 4, run through the same bootstrap
     decision rule ("90% CI lower bound > 0", 10,000 resamples) the harness
     applies to real data: per-gameweek differences drawn Normal(Δ, s) with
     s = 0.10, Δ = 0.03, n = ceil(G) gameweeks → the effect is detected in
     80% ± 5% of replications; with Δ = 0 → detected in 5% ± 2%. A miss is
     reported as a finding and fixed only if it is a bug, never by moving
     the bars.
  2. The 2026/27 reader returns exactly `fpl_edge.forecasts(read_log(), d)`
     (same rows, same count) on a log with two pre-deadline snapshots, one
     post-deadline snapshot and a tie.
  3. Lockdown: a 2026/27 gameweek with `data_checked = 0`, or with one
     fixture `finished = 0`, is never graded (refusal printed).
  4. Leak test: the 2025/26 rebuild for gameweek g changes by nothing when
     rows with `round >= g` are altered, and goes red when a mutation lets
     the training slice include round g (verifier check 1a, three gameweeks).
  5. The rebuilt 2025/26 `bottomup` and `minutes` equal `fpl_hist.backtest_week`
     on a toy season, within 1e-9.
  6. Hand-computed cases: Spearman (equals `_spearman`), Kendall tau-b with
     ties, top-k regret (k = 1, 3, 11), MAE, Brier, log loss, the decision
     metric on a fixed 15 (formation legality, captain doubled, missing
     forecast = 0), the 2025/26 most-selected squad (quotas, club cap,
     budget; equals brute force on a small world), Holm and BH on a
     textbook p-value list, the power formula on a hand case.
  7. `python tools/backtest.py --compare own bottomup --season 2025-26
     --filter starters` prints the "not rebuildable" refusal for `own`;
     `--compare bottomup minutes --season 2025-26 --filter starters`,
     `--season 2026-27`, `--report`, `--power` and `--selftest` run.
  8. The tool writes nothing under the data folder and nothing outside
     `--out` (default `tools/out/`, gitignored).
  9. Nightly isolation (FAST item 1): `fpl_nightly.py` and every module it
     imports import nothing under `tools/` or `analysis/`.
  10. Tests in `tests/test_S4.py`; `python test_fpl.py` count unchanged
      before and after (lane B never edits it).

Every correlation and every difference is printed with its interval; a
missing input is reported and the run stops; nothing is substituted.

No re-tuning after viewing results.
