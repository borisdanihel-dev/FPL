- **Item:** S8 (BUILD_PLAN.md Step 8, item T1; the ruling below scopes it).
- **Hypothesis:** a rolling-horizon planner (0–2 transfers a week, decayed
  six-week xP gain net of hits and lost banked-transfer value) beats the
  one-week greedy baseline over a season by at least 10 points per squad.
- **Data and sample filter:**
  - *Live:* the newest dated export in `C:\GIT\FPL-data` (our 15 with
    selling prices, the bank, `fixtures_next6`, statuses) and the
    projection log through the S2 reader rule: xP = `own` — the recorded
    row for the next gameweek, `fpl_edge.project()` on each later
    fixture-known gameweek (as S7), through the recording gate; a blank or
    gated-out player scores 0. Candidates: 0, 1 or 2 transfers from the top
    25 per position by decayed six-week xP, exhaustive over that pool (no
    MILP): every single swap and every pair of swaps, within the selling
    budget (bank + selling prices), the club cap of 3 and Boris's cap of 2
    GK+DEF per club (Part C rule 11), each candidate's best legal XI and
    captain re-optimised per gameweek (`fpl_edge._best_xi`, captain =
    highest xP). Free transfers banked: `estimate_free_transfers`'s rule
    from the export; never left at 5.
  - *Replay (ruling 4):* 2025/26 GW2–38 on S7's 50 synthetic squads
    (`chips.sample_squads`, seed 7, GW1 ownership, budget and position
    rules); 1 free transfer a week, bank of up to 5, no chips. Forecasts:
    `project_reliability` point-in-time (S4 harness, training rounds < t,
    clubs of the origin by `hist_pit`, the fixture pairings of weeks
    t..t+5 known in advance), as in S7's replay; prices of the origin round
    and selling prices by the FPL rule. Each week the XI and captain by
    forecast, automatic substitutions by `fpl_edge.rule_autosubs` on
    realised minutes (bench in forecast order), hits −4 per extra transfer.
    GW2 is the first origin (one training round): the replay says so.
  - *Decayed xP gain:* ΔxP_t = (candidate XI + captain xP − current XI +
    captain xP) in gameweek t of the six-week horizon, t = 0..5; forecasts
    beyond the known fixtures are not invented — the horizon is the
    fixture-known gameweeks (six live; the replay knows every pairing).
- **Comparison:** the planner's primary configuration against the baseline
  "the best one-week move with the free transfer when its xP gain > 0,
  else roll; never a hit", paired per squad over the whole replay.
- **Primary metric (one):** season points per squad net of hits (realised
  points of the weekly XI + captain, with autosubs, minus 4 × hits),
  planner minus baseline; decay 0.85, Boris's free-transfer schedule
  (1.5 / 1.0 / 0.6 / 0.3 for the 1st..4th banked transfer), hit buffer 1.5
  (a hit only if the decayed gain ≥ 4 + 1.5 = 5.5).
- **Secondary metrics (information only):** the solver schedule (2.1 / 1.8
  / 1.5 / 1.1); decay 0.80 and 0.90 with a flag where the chosen plan
  disagrees with 0.85 (ruling 2, robustness); hits taken per squad;
  transfers made; the planner's gain split into XI points and hits.
- **Bootstrap:** unit = squad (one replay per squad spans the season; the
  deviation from the template's gameweek unit as in S7), 10,000 resamples,
  90% CI (percentile), paired on the per-squad difference, seed 20261018.
  Limitation: the 50 squads share one season.
- **Pass threshold:** mean paired gain ≥ +10 season points per squad net
  of hits AND the 90% CI lower bound > 0 (ruling 4). Other configurations
  are exploratory only. Adoption of any plan as a decision input needs
  Boris's acceptance in chat (ruling 5).
- **Acceptance tests (in addition):**
  1. Score = Σ_{t=0..5} 0.85^t ΔxP_t − 4 × hits − lost banked-FT value
     (hand case); the banked-FT value from the schedule in force: using a
     transfer when k are banked loses the k-th value; never leaving 5
     banked (a mutation that allows it → red).
  2. The hit rule: a hit only if the decayed gain ≥ 5.5, not resting on one
     fixture (the gain without its largest single-gameweek term is still
     > 0), and every incoming player's P(start) ≥ 0.8 (`minutes` row live;
     rebuilt P(start) in the replay) — one mutation each.
  3. Candidates exhaustive over the pool: on a toy pool the planner's best

     club cap 3 and the 2 GK+DEF per club cap each refuse an illegal
     candidate (mutation each).
  4. The replay is point-in-time: forecasts at origin t unchanged when
     every round ≥ t row is altered; a transfer planted at t+1 leaves t
     unchanged (clubs of the origin); the baseline never takes a hit; a
     mutation training through t → red.
  5. Robustness: decay 0.80 and 0.90 reruns and the disagreement flag; the
     price line prints "price predictor: no source" and never a number.
  6. CLI `python tools/planner.py` writes `tools/out/planner_live.txt` (the
