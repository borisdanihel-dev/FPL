- **Data and sample filter:** 2025/26 through the S4 harness (`canonical_at`,

- **Definitions pinned:** per player-match, mean μ = shrunk per-90 rate ×
  90 × opponent factor; the rate = the season's action count / minutes ×
  90 over the training rounds, shrunk toward the positional median (180+
  minutes) by the training slice's measured reliability weight for DEFCON
  (`reliability_inputs`, as bottomup does); opponent factor =
  λ_opp(fixture) / the mean λ over the round's fixtures (more expected
  opposition attacking, more defensive actions; the λ of S5, fallback
  labelled). Dispersion r per position, fitted on the training rounds by
  the method of moments on the model's own means (r = Σμ² / (Σ(x − μ)² −

  or below the mean). P(DEFCON) = P(X ≥ threshold) under NB(μ, r); expected

  `fpl_edge.defcon_expectation`): the player's observed hit rate over his

  for forwards and for a player without such a match, as the code does).

- **Primary metric (one):** Brier score of P(DEFCON) against the realised

- **Secondary metrics (information only):** log loss; the calibration

## Ruling (verbatim)

RULING Step 13 (DEFCON probability):
Negative binomial per player-match: mean = shrunk per-90 rate of the season's DEFCON action count x 90 x opponent factor from lambda; dispersion fitted per position on training rounds. P(DEFCON) = P(count >= the season's threshold for the position); expected points = 2 x P. Sample: player-matches with 60+ minutes (separates DEFCON from minutes). Test on 2025/26 v the current hit-rate approach: Brier better by >= 0.005 with the 90% CI lower bound > 0, and observed v predicted hit rate within 5 percentage points in at least 8 of 10 deciles. State the thresholds used.
END RULINGS
