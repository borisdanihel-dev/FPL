Descriptive: no adoption bar; nothing changes a live decision.
1. Rivals = every other manager in league 455565; gameweek points net of hits; settled gameweeks only (lockdown rule). sigma_i = SD of i's points; d_j = ours - j's; sigma_d,j = SD of d_j; rho_ij of points. Primary: pooled sigma_d = root mean square of sigma_d,j over all rivals (too few gameweeks per pair); per-pair as information. 90% CIs by bootstrap over gameweeks (B = 10,000, fixed seed). sigma_d without chip weeks as information.
2. P(we finish above j) = Phi(-gap_j / (sigma_d * sqrt(n))), gap_j = j's total - ours, n = gameweeks left, zero drift (primary); a drift version from relative xP over the next 6 gameweeks as information. Variance line per rival: gap_j > sigma_d * sqrt(n), printed "beyond the line" or "inside".
3. Threat set = rivals ahead of us; if we lead, rivals with P(j above us) >= 0.05. Weight w_j = P(j finishes above us), normalised. League EO per player = sum of w_j * multiplier_j (captain 2, triple captain 3, starter 1, bench 0; bench 1 under Bench Boost). Rivals' picks for the coming gameweek are hidden until its deadline: the pre-deadline table uses each rival's last settled picks and says so. relative xP = (our multiplier - EO) * xP, xP = own. "Free variance" flag when the xP difference between two options is under 0.3.
4. Captain table: every XI player ranked by own xP, any position; the differential allowance (<= 0.5 xP before GW25, <= 1.0 from GW25) against the threat-set EO; a running count of how often a DEF or GK tops it.
5. Tests: a toy league with known sigma and Phi values; point-in-time (settled gameweeks only, picks at or before the deadline); EO multipliers including TC and BB; a mutation for each. Verifier on Fable as usual.
