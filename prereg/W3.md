## Design (pre-registered)

### (1) Source `xp_v2`

- **New runner module `fpl_xpv2.py`.** It holds the live functions of
  `tools/xp_v2_live.py` (`core`, `export_rows`, `ict_live`,
  `target_fixtures`, `forecast`) and their closure in
  `tools/xp_v2_replay.py`, `tools/shares.py` and `tools/xp_v2.py`. They
  are copied verbatim by AST, as W2 did. The odds functions come from
  `fpl_lambda.py`, which is already S5's code verbatim (W2). The runner
  never imports `tools/`.
- **Inputs; the record never opens the database:**
  - the night's export;
  - the newest Odds API snapshot taken before the deadline, as
    `bottomup_lambda` uses. With no snapshot, every fixture is on the
    fallback, as in `tools/xp_v2_live.py`'s `live()`, and the record line
    says so;
  - the fallback, fitted on the export's results before the deadline
    (`fpl_lambda.results_before`, W2);
  - last season's S11 prior, from a committed file (below);
  - the prior's key per player, from the export: the player's `code`
    gives the 2025/26 name through the file, otherwise the 2026/27 first
    name + second name. This is the rule in `tools/xp_v2_live.py`'s
    `prior_names`.
- **The prior file `priors/s11_prior_2025-26.json`** (on master, about
  100 KB):
  - It is generated offline by `tools/make_prior.py` from
    `historical/2025-26` (`merged_gw.csv`, `teams.csv`,
    `players_raw.csv`), and records each input's SHA-256.
  - It holds S11's `Prior` state exactly: per (name, club), the sums of
    xg, xa, team xg, team xa and minutes, with the position, in S11's
    order; k and rel per metric; and the code → 2025/26 name map.
  - JSON floats round-trip exactly.
  - The runner's `Prior` rebuilds the club-position and league-position
    lists with S11's own code.
  - **This file is a design choice, open for Boris before the merge:**
    the runner has no `historical/` folder, and the record never opens
    the database.
- **The export:** `all_players` gains `code`, `first_name` and
  `second_name`, from `players` (W2 stores `code`). The schema stays 2;
  the keys are only added.
- **The record:**
  - `xp_v2` is recorded for the next gameweek, the value rounded to 3
    places like every source.
  - `SOURCES` gains `xp_v2`, the seventh source, and check 29 learns it.
  - A missing or unreadable prior file, an export without `code`, or a
    player without a key is a RECORD SKIP: a WARN in check 29, never a
    red night.
- **Acceptance:**
  - (a) On the same inputs, `fpl_xpv2.forecast` returns exactly what
    `tools/xp_v2_live.forecast` returns: the same keys, `==` on every float
    and the same detail. The inputs are a toy case, and the real GW8
    export, snapshot and historical files where present.
  - (b) The runner's `Prior` from the file answers every (name, pos,
    club, metric) query, and gives k, exactly as `shares.Prior` does on the
    files: on toy files, and on the real 2025/26 files where present.
    `tools/make_prior.py` regenerates the committed file byte for byte.
  - (c) The record never opens the database. The nightly imports nothing
    under `tools/`. Both are tests.
  - (d) The six other sources' rows are unchanged. This is checked by
    regenerating the log on a copy, with a frozen clock, and diffing it.
    It is also checked at simulated GW10, 20 and 38.

### (2) Report sections (in `fpl_sections.py`, after CHIPS and LEAGUE; the SKIPPED and WARN rules of W2, amendment 2 included)

- **CAPTAIN (Step 14):**
  - Our 15 are the export's latest squad (`chips.my_squad`). xp_v2's XI
    and captain are picked as in `tools/gw_pass.py` (`chips.xi_and_bench`;
    the captain is the highest xp_v2 in the XI, ties to the lower id).
  - The section prints the S14 captain table for our 15 (mean, P(>=10),
    P(>=15) and p90, from `points_pmf.live_pmfs` on the same xp_v2
    forecast), and the options within 0.3 xP of the captain in xp_v2's XI
    (S14 amendment 1).
  - The functions are copied verbatim by AST into a runner module,
    `fpl_pmf.py`.
  - Acceptance: on the same inputs, the lines equal the matching lines of
    `tools/gw_pass.py` section 7.
- **BAN RISK (S12's rule), for our 15:**
  - Cautions are the yellow cards this season in league matches kicked
    off before the deadline (`player_gw.yellow`, fpl.sqlite read-only, as
    S12's `rows_2627`).
  - A player is one booking from a ban when his cautions equal 4 and the
    club's next league match is its 18th or earlier, or equal 9 and that
    match is its 31st or earlier. A booking there would be the 5th before
    the 19th, or the 10th before the 32nd. This is S12's `suspended_next`
    band, moved one match on.
  - Per-90 booking rate: his cautions / (minutes / 90), shrunk m/(m + 4)
    toward the position mean (S12's `booking_rate90`).
    - The position mean is pinned here, because S12 left it open: the
      position's cautions / (minutes / 90), pooled over every player of
      the position this season before the deadline.
  - P(ban for the following gameweek) = min(1, rate90 × P(start)), where
    P(start) is the one xp_v2 as tested uses (the current model's;
    S12's v2 is not adopted).
  - The line lists those players with cautions, rate90 and P(ban); none →
    one line saying so.
- If any input is missing, the section is one SKIPPED line. Nothing is
  substituted.

  `fpl_edge.forecasts`.
- Acceptance: on the same inputs, the numbers equal S6c's `info_2627`.
