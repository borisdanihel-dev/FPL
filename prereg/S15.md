## Design (pinned here; the choices F-G leaves open are marked "pinned")

- **The xP matrix (both sides)** at origin t: xp_v2 as tested for weeks
  u = t..min(t+5, 38), from rounds < t.
  - It is S8b's horizon season (`tools/planner_2425.py`, `Season2425`),
    generalised to any season's folders.
  - Week t uses S5's lambdas (odds, else the fallback), as S6b. Weeks
    t+1..t+5 use the fallback fitted on results before the origin. The
    source per fixture is recorded.
  - 2025/26: priors 2024/25, odds E0 2025/26.
  - 2024/25: priors 2023/24, odds E0 2024/25 with S6f's Ipswich alias.
- **The objective** (the builder's, and the "projected xP" of both
  squads): Σ_{k=0..5} 0.85^k × V_k, where V_k is week t+k's value of the
  15. V_k is:
  - the best legal XI (any of the 7 formations) by that week's xP;
  - plus the highest XI xP again (the captain, from any position);
  - plus S7's bench weights: 0.30 / 0.10 / 0.03 on the outfield bench in
    forecast order, and 0.03 on the sub keeper.
  - A player without a fixture scores 0 that week.
- **The builder** (stdlib, no MILP):
  - Greedy: the best decayed xP per million into each slot, under the
    budget and the club limits, then the cheapest fill.
  - Local search: every single swap (out, in) at the same position, and
    every pair of swaps, taking the best improvement until none improves.
  - Multi-start: from the greedy squad, and from the rule's squad when the
    rule's squad meets the builder's constraints. Local search only
    improves, so with the rule's squad as a start the builder's projected
    xP is ≥ the rule's by construction (BUILD_PLAN's invariant: a shortfall
    is a bug).
- **Constraints:** budget 100.0 at that gameweek's prices; quota 2/5/5/3;
  ≤ 3 per club.
  - **The GK+DEF 2-per-club cap (pinned):** off for both sides in the A/B
    (the frozen rule has no such cap, and the invariant needs like for
    like).
  - The cap's cost is reported every time: the builder with the cap on,
    its projected and realised points against the builder without it.
  - Live, the cap is on (Boris's choice, BUILD_PLAN rule 11), and its cost
    is printed.
- **The frozen three-layer rule** on history, ported to the replay's
  inputs, with a test that the port equals `fpl_edge.wildcard_candidates`
  on a real export when given the same matrix:
  - layer 1: status 'a', mins_last4 ≥ 45, the frozen minutes share >
    0.667 over the team's matches in the last four gameweeks, a fixture in
    the horizon;
  - layer 3: the order. GK/DEF by the six-week xP sum, which is the
    matrix: xp_v2, the same as the builder's (pinned; the live rule's
    bottomup sum6 is replaced by the shared matrix). MID/FWD by P(start) ×
    fixtures, then xGI/90 (the rule's own, model-free).
  - Layer 2's role edges (pens, corners, fk, defcon, arb) only print and
    never rank or admit (frozen code), so they are left out. 2025/26 and
    2024/25 have no point-in-time set-piece orders.
  - Every player is taken as status 'a', as own_rebuilt did (no historical
    status).
  - The squad comes from the frozen `fpl_edge.build_three_layer_squad`,
    called unchanged, formation 4-3-3, with the split from
    `fpl_edge.budget_split` with no current squad: the quota-proportional
    split (pinned: a hypothetical wildcard has no squad).
- **Dates:** every gameweek t = 5..38 of 2025/26 and of 2024/25, each a
  hypothetical wildcard at round t's prices. The window is weeks
  t..min(t+5, 38).
- **Realised points:** in each week of the window, the XI by the origin's
  matrix (any legal formation), the captain = the highest xp_v2 in the XI
  (doubled), `rule_autosubs` on realised minutes (bench in forecast order),
  summed over the window.
  - Outcomes: 2025/26 re-scored under the 2026/27 table by Step 10 (DEFCON
    from its counts); 2024/25 by S10b (DEFCON excluded, no counts).
- **Primary metric (one):** per date, realised window points (builder −
  rule).
  - The dates of both seasons are pooled (pinned; 68 dates), and each
    season is reported alone as information.
- **Bootstrap:** unit = date, 10,000 resamples, seed 20261018, 90% CI.
  - Windows overlap, so dates are correlated (stated). Information only:
    the same on every sixth date (non-overlapping windows).
- **Pass (all three):**
  - the mean difference > 0 with the 90% CI excluding 0;
  - the builder ≥ the rule on ≥ 60% of dates;
  - the builder's projected xP ≥ the rule's on every date.
- **Secondary (information only):**
  - the share of dates where the builder's best week-t formation is not
    4-3-3, and the projected cost of 4-3-3 there;
  - bench points under a Bench Boost in the window's first week;
  - the GK+DEF cap's cost;
  - the per-season results;
  - the lambda sources.
- **Live:** `tools/builder.py --wildcard | --freehit | --bb` from the data
  folder (read-only):
  - `--wildcard`: six-week decayed;
  - `--freehit`: one week, no decay, the budget = bank + selling prices;
  - `--bb`: one week, the bench at full weight.
  - Each prints the best 15 at selling prices, the cap's cost and the
    formation. It is information only until the A/B passes and Boris
    accepts.

## Amendment 1: the local search's candidates (pinned before code, 20 Oct)

Pinned before any S15 code, because every pair of swaps over every player
is too slow for 136 builds.

- **Candidates for a swap:** S8's pool, the top 25 per position by
  decayed six-week xP, and the 15 themselves.
- **Single swaps:** best improvement, repeated until none improves.
- **Then pair swaps:** first improvement, in a fixed order: by position,
  then by the pool's order. Taking one sends the search back to single
  swaps. It stops when no single swap and no pair improves by more than
  1e-9.
- **The greedy start** draws from every player who passes the gate, not
  only the pool.
- Nothing else changes.
