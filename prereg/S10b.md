# S10b — Step 10b: 2023/24 and 2024/25 re-scored under the 2026/27 rules

Committed before any S10b download or code. The ruling is item F-C of RULINGS
PART F (19 Oct 2026), verbatim at the end; the whole block is in BUILD_PLAN.md
"Part F - queued rulings". Offline under `tools/`; the nightly imports
nothing of it. Never edited after any of its evaluation numbers exist.

- **Item:** S10b (Part F, F-C; BUILD_PLAN.md Step 10 extended to two more
  seasons). `tools/rules.py` gains a season argument; tests in
  `tests/test_S10b.py`.
- **Hypothesis (the claim under test, as Step 10's):** each season's own
  points table is the 2026/27 table (`historical/2026-27/game_config.json`,
  `scoring`) without the defensive-contribution category, since DEFCON
  points began in 2025/26. If so, scoring the season with that table under
  its own positions reproduces FPL's official points. No other source for
  the 2023/24 and 2024/25 tables exists in the repository or the downloads,
  so the reproduction test is what checks the claim. A category it
  contradicts (e.g. a goalkeeper goal worth other than the table's value)
  shows in the per-category listing of mismatches and is reported, never
  patched after the results.
- **Data (downloads approved in F-C):**
  - vaastav `data/2022-23`, `2023-24`, `2024-25`: `gws/merged_gw.csv`,
    `fixtures.csv`, `teams.csv`, `players_raw.csv`;
  - football-data `mmz4281/2324/E0.csv` and `2425/E0.csv`, used by later
    items (F-D, F-E) for pre-closing odds only.
  - Each file is recorded with its URL, fetch time (UTC), size and SHA-256
    in `worklog/downloads_2026-10-19b.md`, kept under `historical/<season>/`
    (gitignored) or `tools/odds/` (the football-data files, as S5's), and
    read only by Python run with `-I`. Never executed or sourced. A file
    already local (2024-25's `merged_gw.csv`, `fixtures.csv`, `teams.csv`
    from S11) is compared with the download by SHA-256, and a difference is
    reported.
- **Definitions pinned:**
  - *Reproduction test, per season:* every player-gameweek of the season's
    `merged_gw.csv`, manager rows (position `AM`) out, scored with the
    season's own table (above) under the season's own position, against
    `total_points`, within ±1, among players who appeared (minutes > 0).
    The primary share is the share within ±1, with Step 10's 90%
    gameweek-bootstrap CI (10,000 resamples, seed 20261019). A per-category
    listing of the mismatches is printed (Step 10's secondary lines).
  - *The re-score, per season:* every player-gameweek scored with the
    2026/27 table under the player's 2026/27 position by `code` (2026/27
    `elements_positions.csv`; the season's `players_raw.csv` for its code),
    else his own position, flagged. Bonus stays official, flagged "<season>
    BPS". DEFCON comes from its components where the season's rows hold
    the defensive-action counts; where they do not, it is 0 and flagged
    `DEFCON missing` on every row. Assists are rescaled only if the table
    shows a change, as Step 10. Output: one row per player-gameweek with
    every category, written by `python tools/rules.py normalise --season
    <season> OUT.csv`.
- **Comparison:** the re-score against the official points, per category,
  information only.
- **Primary metric (one per season):** the reproduction share.
- **Secondary metrics (information only):** the mismatches by category and
  position; the re-score's total change per category; the count of
  players reclassified by code; the count of rows without a 2026/27 code.
- **Bootstrap:** unit = gameweek, 10,000 resamples, 90% CI, seed 20261019.
- **Pass threshold:** the primary share ≥ 0.99 for each season separately
  (BUILD_PLAN.md Step 10). A season that fails is not used by later items
  until the chat rules.
- **Acceptance tests:** the season argument reads the right files; manager
  rows out; the own table drops only the defensive-contribution category; a
  hand row scored under each table; DEFCON missing flagged, never invented;
  the 2026/27 position by code, and a player without a code flagged; bonus
  official; the reproduction share and the bootstrap on a toy; the 2025/26
  path byte-identical to Step 10's output (`normalise` and `test`
  unchanged for 2025/26); `run.txt`; nightly isolation; `test_fpl.py`
  untouched.

No re-tuning after viewing results.
