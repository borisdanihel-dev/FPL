# S7 — Step 7 (P4, first-half chip calculator and Free Hit screen)

Committed before any code for this item. Lane B, offline work:
`tools/chips.py` is never imported by the nightly; wiring it in is lane A's

- **Item:** S7 (BUILD_PLAN.md Step 7, item P4; offline part).
- **Hypothesis:** a backward-induction chip calculator, fed point-in-time
  forecasts, times Bench Boost 1 and Free Hit 1 at least as well as the
  heuristic (BB on the highest forecast bench week, FH on the weakest
  forecast XI week), measured in realised chip points over the first half.
- **Data and sample filter:**
  - *Live use (2026/27):* the newest export in `C:\GIT\FPL-data` (our 15,
    their `selling_price`, the bank, `fixtures_next6`, `events`) and the
    projection log through `fpl_edge.forecasts()` (S2 reader rule). xP source:
    `own` (ruling 1) — the recorded `own` row for the next gameweek, and for
    later gameweeks `fpl_edge.project()` from the same export on that
    gameweek's fixture(s) (the recording formula; a test checks it equals
    the recorded row for the next gameweek). Beyond six gameweeks the export
    has no fixtures, so weeks 7+ are labelled "proxy" and carry the
    six-week-ahead value of the latest fixture-known week. Budget = bank +
    selling prices (`fpl_edge.selling_budget`, S2 ruling 2); refused, never
    read from `value`, when the export lacks them.
  - *Test replay (2025/26, ruling 4):* `historical/2025-26/` through
    `fpl_hist.canonical_from_vaastav()`; forecasts are the S4 rolling-origin
    rebuild of `bottomup` (the only xP source that can be rebuilt for
    2025/26 under S4 ruling 2; `own` cannot, so the replay uses `bottomup`
    for every comparator alike). At origin t the forecast for week u >= t is
    `project_reliability` trained on rounds 1..t-1 on week u's fixture
    pairings (known in advance, results not), as the horizon test did.
    Origins GW5–19 (GW1–4 have no training window): chips can be played
    GW5–19, the same window for every comparator. Residuals (point-in-time)
    come from the harness's graded weeks < t.
  - *Synthetic squads:* 50 squads, seed 7 (`random.Random(7)`), sampled
    from GW1 ownership: players drawn without replacement with probability
    proportional to `selected` on the GW1 rows, accepted when the 15 obey
    2 GK / 5 DEF / 5 MID / 3 FWD, at most 3 per club and cost <= £100.0m at
    GW1 `value` (rejection sampling, position quotas filled in draw order).
    Squads are held fixed GW5–19 (no transfers; the same for every
    comparator). Selling price at week t = `fpl_edge.selling_price(buy at
    GW1, value at t)`; the bank is £100.0m minus the GW1 cost.
  - *2026/27 residuals (live uncertainty, ruling 2):* the per-player
    residual (realised minus forecast) of the xP source in use, by position,
    over every gameweek the S4 harness has graded for it (`own`: 2026/27
    GW4 onward); the count per position is printed.
- **Comparison:** calculator vs heuristic (primary); calculator vs "both as
  early as possible" (BB GW5, FH GW6; information only). All three decide at
  each week t from the same point-in-time forecasts for t..19 and the same
  fixed squad. Heuristic at week t: play BB if BB is unused and the forecast
  bench xP (four players, weight 1) at t is >= that of every week t+1..19;
  play FH if FH is unused, t != 19, no chip already played at t, and the
  forecast FH gap at t is >= that of every later week.
- **Primary metric (one):** realised chip points per squad = BB gain + FH
  gain, where BB gain = realised bench points (the four) minus the realised
  automatic-substitution points that would have come on without BB
  (`fpl_edge.rule_autosubs` on realised minutes, bench ordered by forecast),
  and FH gain = realised points of the FH XI (best legal 15 and XI under the
  week's selling budget, chosen pre-deadline by forecast through the item N
  solver; captain doubled) minus realised points of our best forecast XI
  that week (captain doubled). A chip not played by GW19 scores 0.
- **Secondary metrics (information only):** the gameweeks chosen by each
  policy; the split BB / FH; the mean margin PLAY vs HOLD and its interval
  from the residual resampling; the "both early" comparison.
- **Bootstrap:** unit = squad here (one replay per squad spans the whole
  half, so the squad is the independent unit; stated as a deviation from the
  template's gameweek unit and why), 10,000 resamples, 90% CI (percentile),
  paired on the per-squad difference calculator minus heuristic, seed
  20261018. Limitation stated up front: the 50 squads share one season, so
  common shocks are not resampled away.
- **Pass threshold:** mean paired gain (calculator minus heuristic) >= 0
  points AND the 90% CI lower bound > -3 points (BUILD_PLAN Step 7 item 6).
- **Acceptance tests (in addition):**
  1. Chip values. V_BB,t = Σ over the four bench players of xP_t × (1 − w),
     w = 0.30 / 0.10 / 0.03 for the outfield bench in order and 0.03 for the
     sub keeper (the autosub contribution that happens anyway); in the
     planned BB week w = 1.0 for the bench, so V_BB there is the full bench
     xP. V_FH,t = best legal XI for week t under the selling budget (item N
     solver, `free_hit_table` machinery) minus our best XI by the same
     metric. No transfer planner exists yet (Step 8): "our XI after normal
     transfers" is our best XI from the current 15, stated in the output as
     an upper bound on V_FH; the Step 8 hook is a function argument.
  2. Backward induction over (t, chips left ⊆ {BB, FH}), t = next gameweek
     .. 19, terminal value 0 at t = 20: V(t, S) = max over hold, play BB
     (if in S), play FH (if in S and t != 19) of the chip's value plus the
     continuation E[V(t+1, S')]; one chip per gameweek. Output: the V table
     per week, the continuation values, and PLAY BB / PLAY FH / HOLD for the
     next gameweek. The decision uses the forecast means; 1,000 residual
     resamples by position give the interval on the next-week margin and the
     share of resamples with the same action. Hand-computed cases: a
     three-week toy where the exact optimum is known; FH never at 19; both
     chips worth 0 after 19; no two chips in one week.
  3. Bench prep: for the planned BB week, for each bench slot the best
     same-position replacement within the selling budget by that week's xP
     (one transfer, no hit), with the gain in V_BB; information only.
  4. Free Hit screen (ruling 5 = Step 7 item 5): per future gameweek the gap
     between the best legal one-week XI under the selling budget and ours,
     flagged above 8 or when 3+ of our XI are not status `a`; reuses item
     N's solver and flags (`FH_GAP`, `FH_NOT_A`); a test shows the budget is
     the selling budget, not `value`.
  5. The replay: the 50 squads are legal and reproducible from seed 7; a
     forecast at origin t never reads a row with round >= t (mutation red);
     the heuristic's and the calculator's chips obey the one-per-week and
     FH != 19 rules; realised gains hand-checked on a toy week.
  6. `python tools/chips.py` (live, read-only on the data copy, writes under
     `--out`) and `python tools/chips.py --replay` run; nightly isolation
     test as S4; tests in `tests/test_S7.py`.

No re-tuning after viewing results.
