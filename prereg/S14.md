## Open question before the rows (2026-10-19)

"The PMF mean must match xp_v2 within 0.05", but xp_v2 (S6b as tested;
`tools/xp_v2_replay.py`, `tools/xp_v2_live.py`) does not hold every
component the ruling lists:

- **saves:** in neither half (a keeper's 1 point per 3 saves is not in
  xp_v2);
- **goals conceded:** in the bottomup half only (−E[floor(GC/2)] for GK and
  DEF); the xg half has none;
- **bonus:** in the xg half only (0.8 × the ICT percentile); the bottomup
  half has none;
- **DEFCON:** the hit-rate rule in the xg half, the logistic in the bottomup
  half;
- **appearance:** 2 points per start in both halves (no 1-point start
  under 60 minutes); the sub term is the empirical E[points | sub] per
  position.

So a PMF that convolves the listed components at their own measured rates
(saves, goals conceded and an empirical bonus for everyone) cannot match
xp_v2's mean within 0.05: keepers miss by about their saves points. The
options as I see them:

- **(a)** The PMF is the distribution of xp_v2 itself: P(start) × (½ PMF of
  the xg half + ½ PMF of the bottomup half) + P(sub) × the empirical
  sub-points PMF + the rest at 0. Within each half every term becomes a
  distribution with that term's mean (goals and assists Poisson at the
  half's rates, clean sheet Bernoulli, goals conceded from Poisson(λ_opp)
  where the half has it, DEFCON Bernoulli at the half's rate, bonus shaped
  empirically conditional on returns and scaled to the half's bonus mean);
  saves enter neither half, as xp_v2 has none. The 0.05 check then guards
  the implementation (it holds by construction).
- **(b)** Every listed component at its own measured rate for every player
  (saves included); the 0.05 check is reported as it falls and will fail
  for keepers: a new model, not xp_v2's distribution.
- **(c)** (a) plus saves added: xp_v2's mean moves by the saves term, which
  is a change to the accepted model and needs its own test.

Recommendation: (a). Nothing is built until the chat answers.

## Template rows (filled 2026-10-19 after RULING 19 Oct afternoon point 1, before any S14 code; the rulings above govern where they differ)

- **Item:** S14 (BUILD_PLAN.md Step 14, item N3). `tools/points_pmf.py`;
  tests in `tests/test_S14.py`; the bundle gains a captain table
  (information only). Branch `s14-wip` from `landing-2026-10-19b` (it needs
  S6b's replay and S6d's live xp_v2).
- **Hypothesis:** the points distribution of xp_v2 as tested predicts 10+
  hauls better than the per-position haul rate, and its expected count of
  hauls is within ±15% of the actual count.
- **Data and sample filter:** 2025/26 GW5–38 through S6b's replay (xp_v2 as
  tested, point-in-time: `canonical_at`, clubs of the origin, training
  rounds < t, S5's λ, S11's priors and k). Population = every
  player-gameweek with an xp_v2 forecast at origin t (S6b's gate: 45+
  minutes in the last four and a fixture). Outcome = the re-scored points
  of round t (`points_2627_rules`, Step 10; 0 without a row; a double
  gameweek one unit).
- **Definitions pinned (the distribution of xp_v2 itself; its mean is
  xp_v2 by construction):**
  - *PMF* of a player-gameweek = P(start) × (½ X + ½ L) + P(sub) × U +
    (1 − P(start) − P(sub)) × δ0: start, sub and no appearance exclusive,
    the two halves a 50/50 mixture. P(start), P(sub) and E[pts | sub] are
    xp_v2's own. If P(start) + P(sub) > 1 (possible only where a double
    gameweek's two fixture rows enter the windows), the start and sub parts
    are added as independent events instead, so the mean still matches; the
    count of such player-gameweeks is printed.
  - *X, the xg half given a start* = 2 + Σ over the gameweek's fixtures of
    (GOAL_VALUE[pos] × G_f + 3 × A_f + CS_VALUE[pos] × C_f) + 2 × D + B:
    - G_f ~ Poisson(xG_f) and A_f ~ Poisson(xA_f): S11's per-fixture
      allocation as S6b computes it;
    - C_f ~ Bernoulli(exp(−λ_opp,f)): S5's λ;
    - D ~ Bernoulli(h), h the player's DEFCON hit rate over his 60+-minute
      training matches (the hit-rate rule; 0 for keepers and forwards);
    - B the bonus.
    Appearance, DEFCON and bonus count once per gameweek, as xp_v2's xg half
    counts them.
  - *L, the bottomup half given a start* = Σ over fixtures of (2 +
    GOAL_VALUE[pos] × G'_f + 3 × A'_f + K_f + 2 × D'_f):
    - G'_f ~ Poisson(0.62 × xgi) and A'_f ~ Poisson(0.38 × xgi), xgi the
      shrunk per-match xGI of `project_reliability`;
    - K_f for GK and DEF: one count GC_f ~ Poisson(λ_opp,f) (S5's λ) gives
      both terms, K = CS_VALUE[pos] × 1{GC = 0} − floor(GC / 2), so clean
      sheet and goals conceded are not independent of each other;
    - K_f for MID: Bernoulli(exp(−λ_own,f)) × 1, with `project_reliability`'s
      own team-rate λ;
    - K_f for FWD: 0;
    - D'_f ~ Bernoulli(p_dc), `project_reliability`'s logistic probability
      (DEF and MID), per fixture as bottomup counts it.
    Poisson tails are cut at 15, as S5's `MAX_GOALS`.
  - *U, the sub points* = the empirical distribution of the official
    2025/26 points of sub appearances (minutes > 0, no start) per position
    in rounds < t (the rows of S6b's E[pts | sub], so its mean is E[pts |
    sub] exactly); δ0 when a position has none.
  - *Bonus, empirical and conditional on returns:*
    - Source: starts in rounds < t (official rows). Live, the same rows come
      from the export (`player_gw_recent`).
    - Cells: group (GK/DEF or MID/FWD) × goal involvements (0, 1, 2+), and
      for GK/DEF also clean sheet (0/1). Each cell holds the share of b ∈ {0,
      1, 2, 3}.
    - Back-off for a cell with fewer than 30 rows: the same group and
      involvements with the clean sheet pooled, then the involvements over
      both groups, then all rows.
    - The player's returns (his goals, assists and clean sheet in the
      gameweek, from X's own counts) pick the cell. The cell distributions
      are then tilted, P_θ(b) ∝ P(b) e^{θb}, with θ found by bisection so
      that the bonus mean over the player's returns equals 0.8 × his ICT
      percentile (xp_v2's bonus term).
    - B = 0 when that target is 0. If no cell has mass above 0 (the target
      cannot be reached), B ~ Bernoulli(target) on {0, 1}, counted and
      printed.
  - *Independence, stated:* within a half, goals, assists, the clean
    sheet (and goals conceded), and DEFCON are independent of each other
    and across fixtures; the bonus depends on the returns only; the start,
    sub and no-appearance cases exclude each other.
  - *Saves:* none (xp_v2 has none; "xp_v2 + saves" is the GW10-checkpoint
    item of RULING 19 Oct afternoon point 1).
  - *Mean check (the ruling's):* |E[PMF] − xp_v2| ≤ 0.05 for every
    player-gameweek; the maximum is printed.
  - *Summaries:* mean, P(≥ 10), P(≥ 15) and p90, the smallest k with
    P(points ≤ k) ≥ 0.9.
- **Comparison:** the Brier score of P(≥ 10) from the PMF against the
  haul-rate baseline: at origin t, per position (the origin's), the share of
  player-gameweeks with re-scored points ≥ 10 over rounds 2..t−1 among
  players with 45+ minutes in their previous four rounds (the same gate;
  outcomes of rounds < t only).
- **Primary metric (one):** per gameweek, the mean over the population of
  (Brier of the baseline − Brier of the PMF) for the event "points ≥ 10".
- **Secondary metrics (information only):** the same for P(≥ 15) against
  the per-position 15+ rate; reliability by decile of P(≥ 10) (predicted
  against observed); the mean check's maximum; the count of
  P(start) + P(sub) > 1 cases; the predicted and actual counts of 15+.
- **Bootstrap:** unit = gameweek, 10,000 resamples of the 34 per-gameweek
  means, 90% percentile interval, seed 20261018 (`backtest.bootstrap_mean`).
- **Pass threshold (both):** the 90% lower bound of the primary metric > 0,
  and the predicted count of 10+ hauls (Σ P(≥ 10) over GW5–38) within ±15%
  of the actual count. Information only until it passes and Boris accepts.
- **Captain table (live, information only):** a new bundle section: for
  our 15, from xp_v2 as tested for the next gameweek, each player's mean,
  P(≥ 10), P(≥ 15) and p90, sorted by mean. The live bonus cells come from
  the export's starts.
- **Acceptance tests:**
  - every PMF sums to 1;
  - its mean equals xp_v2 within 1e-6 on toys and hand cases;
  - the GK/DEF clean sheet and goals conceded come from one Poisson count
    (a hand case);
  - the bonus tilt reaches its target and leaves zero cells at zero;
  - a double gameweek sums its fixtures;
  - the mixture weights, and the independent fallback when P(start) +
    P(sub) > 1;
  - P(≥ 10), P(≥ 15) and p90 on a hand PMF;
  - point-in-time: rows of rounds ≥ t altered leave origin t's PMF and
    baseline unchanged;
  - the pass line needs both conditions;
  - the bundle's captain table;
  - `run.txt`; the CLI; nightly isolation; `test_fpl.py` untouched.

No re-tuning after viewing results.
