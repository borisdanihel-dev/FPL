### The model (part 3)

- **Ratings:** an Elo over the five tiers, fitted on the league results
  before the origin.
  - Each club starts at its first appearance: 1500 in E0, 1400 in E1, 1300
    in E2, 1200 in E3, 1100 in EC. A non-league club not in the data is
    1000.
  - K = 20; home advantage 60 points. Ratings carry over seasons, so
    promotion and relegation link the tiers.
  - Cup CSV names map to football-data names through a pinned table
    (`tools/cups/names.csv`), which a test checks for completeness.
- **A tie:** P(home club advances) = 1 / (1 + 10^(−(R_home + H − R_away) / 400)).
  - H = 60 at a home ground, and 0 at a neutral venue (the FA Cup
    semi-finals and final, the EFL Cup final).
  - The EFL Cup's two-legged semi-final also uses H = 0.
  - Draws, extra time and penalties are inside the advance probability.
- **Future rounds:** a round not yet drawn is drawn uniformly at random
  among the clubs left. Before the neutral rounds, the first club drawn
  is at home.
- **Blank rounds (pinned):** the FA Cup quarter-finals and semi-finals,
  and the EFL Cup final. Each counts only when its date falls in a
  gameweek's window on a day of that gameweek's league fixtures, i.e. a
  league weekend.
  - In such a round, every Premier League fixture of gameweek k (the
    original fixture list) involving a club in the round is postponed.
  - Both of its clubs blank in k.
  - P(club blanks in k) is the inclusion–exclusion over its fixture's two
    clubs: P(club or its opponent in the round). The Monte Carlo estimates
    the joint directly.
- **Doubles, from the table of free midweeks:**
  - A midweek (Tue–Thu) is free for a fixture when neither club has a
    league, cup or European match that week, by the CSVs, the fixture
    list and the simulated progress.
  - A postponed fixture is replayed in the first free midweek after its
    gameweek; with none, the last free midweek before it, after the
    origin.
  - The gameweek whose window holds that midweek is a double for both
    clubs. With no free midweek, no double is predicted, and it is
    counted as such.
- **European progress:** the league phase is played as scheduled.
  - An English club is eliminated at each knockout stage with probability
    0.5. There are no ratings for foreign clubs; this is a stated
    limitation.
  - Matchday dates come from the European CSV.
- **The run:** 20,000 simulations per origin, seed 20261021. For every
  Premier League club and every gameweek after the origin, P(blank) and
  P(double).

### The backtest (part 4)

- **Seasons:** 2024/25 and 2025/26.
- **Origins:** the deadlines of GW10, GW15, GW20, GW25 and GW30 in each
  season.
  - Only what is dated before the origin is used: the league results, the
    draws and the cup results.
  - The original fixture list (the id blocks) is the season's published
    schedule, known before GW1.
  - Postponements announced before the origin are not used.
- **Targets:** every (club, gameweek, origin) with the gameweek after the
  origin's gameweek, up to GW38. Two predictions each: blank (0 fixtures)
  and double (2 or more).
- **The base rate:** the frequency of a team blank, and of a team double,
  per team-gameweek. It is pooled over the part 1 seasons before the test
  season (2018/19 on), excluding 2022/23's void round and 2019/20's
  restart events above 38. Point-in-time: 2025/26's base rate includes
  2024/25.
- **Primary metric:** the Brier skill score, BSS = 1 − BS_model / BS_base,
  over all blank and double predictions of both seasons pooled.
- **Pass:** BSS ≥ 0.10.
- **Information:**
  - BSS for blanks and for doubles apart, and per season;
  - a reliability table (10 bins);
  - a bootstrap 90% interval over (season, target gameweek) blocks,
    B = 10,000, seed 20261021.
- **Weather and other postponements** (2024/25 GW15) stay in the
  outcome; the model cannot see them.
- **No re-tuning:** the Elo constants, the 0.5 European rule, the rounds,
  the double rule, the origins and N are fixed here. Nothing is changed
  after a backtest result is seen. A FAIL is recorded as a FAIL.
