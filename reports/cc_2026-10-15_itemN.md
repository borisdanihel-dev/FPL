## Decisions I made that you may want to change

- **"3+ of our XI not 'a'"** counts our best XI for that week, by today's status. The alternative reading — our picked XI (slots 1–11) — would flag a starter crisis even when the bench covers it.
- **The market uses the recording gate** (status `a`, 45+ minutes); our fifteen is counted as it is.
- **The bench is set aside before the XI**: the cheapest player at each position, lowest metric first, distinct clubs. The XI is exact given that bench; if the cheapest player at a position were also worth starting, he would sit.
- **Budget is the API's team value.** A real Free Hit budget uses selling prices, which are lower for anyone who has risen since we bought him.
