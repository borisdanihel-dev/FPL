## Ruling 2: how I read it, and what the code does

- `snapshot_date` is the UTC date of `made_at`.
- The natural key is `(event, player_id, source, snapshot_date)`. No schema change.
- **Every snapshot day is kept.** A same-day re-run replaces only that day's block for that source and gameweek.
- **Readers take the latest snapshot day** per (source, gameweek), then the last row per key: calibration, `--watch` and the Free Hit screen, through `forecasts()`. Before the deadline they therefore see exactly what the old one-snapshot log held.
- Duplicate keys are reported (`LOG NOTE`) and kept. The real log has 0.
- **`made_at` is the record time**, a few minutes after the sync's fetch. The export's `generated` holds the fetch time. Under "no schema change" this is the only reading available: decision 1.
