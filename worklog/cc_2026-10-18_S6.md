## What was built

`tools/xp_v2.py` (standard library only; the nightly never imports it;
never writes the projection log — tested) and `tests/test_S6.py` (7
tests). Builds on S4's harness (the S2 reader rule, lockdown, our 15,
Spearman, bootstrap, power), S5's `rebuild_2627` and odds book, and
`chips.newest_export`.

- `xp_v2 = P(start) × mean(E_xg[pts | start], E_bl[pts | start]) + P(sub) × E[pts | sub]`
  - P(start) = the recorded `minutes` row (the minutes model; it enters
    only as P(start), never as points — tested by a mutation);
  - E_xg[pts | start] = the recorded `xg` row / P(start) (every term of
    the model scales with P(start), so this recovers the conditional value
    exactly); E_bl[pts | start] = `bottomup_lambda` / P(start), rebuilt
    from the newest export before the deadline with the 2026/27 odds (S5);
    the export's P(start) equals the `minutes` row to 0.000 on both weeks;
  - P(sub) = (appearances − starts) over the same four-gameweek window from
    `player_gw` rows before the deadline, scaled by `chance_next_round`;
  - E[pts | sub] = the mean realised points of sub appearances by position
    over the gameweeks before the deadline — measured (GK had none before
    GW6: 0.00 with n = 0 printed; DEF 1.1, MID 1.2, FWD 1.5).
- No penalty term (amendment 1 point 5a). Candidates: the equal-weight
  blend (primary) and the best single model (`xg_only` or `bl_only`),
  chosen on the development set and frozen (5b); the blend is replaced
  only if the single model wins validation by ≥ 0.02 on the primary metric
  and on decision points.
- Metrics (S4): per-gameweek Spearman among predicted starters
  (`minutes` ≥ 0.8), `xp_v2` − `own`, paired on the same players; the
  decision metric XI + captain from our 15; the paired bootstrap; G from
  the calculator at Δ = 0.02 with s_d from the development set.
- CLI: `--develop --cut N`, `--validate --cut N` (the cut is a frozen
  argument; without it the tool refuses), `--forecast` (next gameweek's
  `xp_v2` from the newest export and the recorded `xg`/`minutes` rows,
  `bottomup_lambda` live; information only).
