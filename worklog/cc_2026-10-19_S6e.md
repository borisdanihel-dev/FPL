## What was tested

For goalkeepers, xp_v2 + saves = xp_v2 as tested + P(start) × E[saves |
start] / 3, where

E[saves | start] = Σ over fixtures of (Z × his saves per 90 + (1 − Z) × the
keepers' mean saves per 90) × (minutes per start / 90) × (λ_opp / the round's
mean λ).

- Z = m / (m + k), with k from S11's split-half rule on 2024/25 keepers.
- The test: on S7's 50 squads, the starting keeper chosen by each model,
  with realised (re-scored) keeper points; if the chosen keeper did not
  play, the other keeper's points.

- The ruled E[saves] / 3 overstates the expected save points. FPL awards
  1 point per full 3 saves, and E[floor(saves / 3)] is below E[saves] / 3;

  close to the MAE rise.
