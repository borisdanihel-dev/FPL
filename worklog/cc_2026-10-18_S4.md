## Amendment 2 — the club-map leak and its fix

The verifier's truncation test showed that `fpl_hist.canonical_from_vaastav`
gives every player the club of his last row in the season and
`fpl_edge.canonical_through` slices rows and matches but not players, so an
early-gameweek rebuild used end-of-season clubs (29 mid-season club changes
in 2025/26, the first five from GW2). RULING S4 amendment 2 (verbatim in
`prereg/S4.md`): the frozen adapter is not edited; `tools/hist_pit.py`
derives a player's club in gameweek g from that gameweek's row (the home
team of its fixture if `was_home`, else the away team), cross-checked
against the row's `team` column (a mismatch stops the run — the real file
has none), carries the last known club over a blank, and never backfills
before a player's first row. `tools/backtest.py` rebuilds gameweek g on
`canonical_at(hist, g)` — the adapter's slice with players replaced by
`hist_pit.players_at(c, cm, g)` (only players who exist by g, each at his
club of rounds ≤ g) — and the decision squad's club cap reads the same map.
Own truncation check after the fix: GW5, GW20 and GW38 rebuilt from copies
truncated to rounds ≤ g give max |difference| 0.0 on every player and the
same most-selected 15. The 2026/27 path is untouched (its report section is
byte-identical before and after).

Numbers computed before the fix stay below, marked **invalid: club-map
leak**, as the ruling requires; the recomputed numbers follow each.

## Limitations stated

- 2025/26 `bottomup`/`minutes` are the historical-rebuild variants (no
  injury flag, no status gate), as the prereg says.
- The 2025/26 decision squad is a fixed context; `selected` of gameweek g-1
  is read from that gameweek's rows (a player with no row in g-1 — a blank —
  is not a candidate for g).
- The decision metric has no autosubs and no vice-captain; a double
  gameweek's P(start) > 1 is skipped in the Brier cell and counted.
- The 2026/27 report is 2–3 gameweeks deep; nothing in it is a finding.
