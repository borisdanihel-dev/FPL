## What was built

`tools/planner.py` (standard library only; the nightly never imports it)
and `tests/test_S8.py` (7 tests). Builds on S7 (`own_week_points`,
`my_squad`, `sample_squads`, the replay's point-in-time forecasts with
clubs of the origin, `xi_and_bench`), S4's harness and `fpl_edge`'s
`estimate_free_transfers`, `selling_budget`, `rule_autosubs`, `forecasts`.

- **xP** = `own`: the recorded row for the next gameweek, `project()` on
  each later fixture-known gameweek (six live), as S7; the replay uses
  `project_reliability` point-in-time (the only 2025/26-rebuildable source).
- **Pool**: the top 25 per position by decayed six-week xP outside our 15.
- **Candidates**: 0 transfers, every single same-position swap, every pair
  of swaps — exhaustive over the pool (34,633 candidates on a replay week;
  the live run evaluates every one in a few seconds) — within the selling
  budget, the club cap of 3 and the 2 GK+DEF per club cap; the best legal
  XI and captain re-optimised per gameweek by a fast exact evaluator
  (sorted projections per position, every formation, captain = the
  highest in the XI; equal to brute force on the toy).
- **Score** = Σ_{t=0..5} 0.85^t ΔxP_t − 4 × hits − lost banked-FT value,
  the FT stock valued by the schedule (Boris 1.5/1.0/0.6/0.3; solver
  2.1/1.8/1.5/1.1; a fifth is worth 0) and the loss measured against
  rolling; rolling at 5 banked is not allowed. A hit only if the decayed
  gain ≥ 5.5, not resting on one fixture (the gain without its largest
  single-gameweek term stays > 0) and every incoming P(start) ≥ 0.8 (the
  `minutes` row live; the rebuilt P(start) in the replay).
- **Robustness**: the choice re-run at decay 0.80 and 0.90; the live table
  flags a different top plan; the replay counts disagreeing weeks on five
  squads.
- **Price line**: "price predictor: no source" (ruling 3).
- **Replay** (ruling 4): 2025/26 GW2–38, S7's 50 squads (seed 7), 1 FT a
  week, bank to 5, no chips; weekly XI and captain by forecast, autosubs by
  `rule_autosubs` on realised minutes, hits −4; baseline = the best
  one-week move with the FT when its gain > 0, else roll, never a hit;
  paired bootstrap over squads (B = 10,000, 90%, seed 20261018).
