## What was built

`tools/calendar_2627.py` (offline; stdlib; read-only on `fpl.sqlite`):
- **Windows:** gameweek g's window is [deadline g, deadline g+1); GW38's is
  open at the end.
- **Dates:** a date is a UK calendar day. BST is coded by rule (01:00 UTC on
  the last Sundays of March and October), with no time-zone database.
- **Mapping:**
  - A day wholly inside one window maps to that gameweek.
  - A day that holds a deadline is named both ways, never a side: the plan
    gives no kickoff times.
  - A day before GW1's deadline raises.
- **Information:** league fixtures that UK day are counted.
