## What was built

`tools/minutes_v2.py` (standard library only; the nightly never imports it)
and `tests/test_S12.py` (7 tests, 4 classes). Builds on S4's harness
(`canonical_at`, `rebuild_2526` for the current model's `p_start`,
`bootstrap_mean`, `MY_ENTRY`'s recording gate) and `tools/hist_pit.py`
(clubs of the origin). The raw 2025/26 rows (per-fixture minutes, starts,
yellow cards, kickoff times) are read from the vaastav copy directly, rounds
< t only; live from the newest dated export and `fpl.sqlite`
(`player_gw.yellow`, `fixtures.kickoff_time`, `players.team_id`).

- **States** (`base_states`): P(start) = starts / matches over the club's
  last min(4, t−1) matches, P(sub) = appearances without a start over the
  same, P(none) the rest; live, scaled by `chance_next_round` as the
  current model is.
- **GK lock** (`keeper_lock`): the keeper with the most starts in the
  club's last 3 matches gets 0.95 unless flagged (live: status not `a`),
  the next 0.05, every other keeper 0; a tie goes to the keeper who
  started the latest match.
- **Cover** (`regulars_and_absent`, `replacement_for`): a regular = started
  ≥ 3 of the club's last 4; out live when flagged, in the replay when he
  played 0 minutes in the previous match after starting the 4 before; his
  replacement = the same-position teammate with the most starts in the
  training-window matches the regular did not start (ties: more starts
  overall, then the lower id); the replacement's P(start) = max(his own,
  the regular's before the absence); none when nobody qualifies.
- **Congestion** (`congestion_factor`): c = start rate of regulars with
  ≤ 3 days' rest / with > 3 days', fitted on the training rounds at every
  origin (league matches only; cups are not in the data), applied as
  P(start) × c capped at 1 for clubs on ≤ 3 days' rest, c = 1 when the 90%
  bootstrap interval over training rounds includes 1 (B = 10,000, seed
  20261018).
- **Suspensions** (`suspended_next`, `p_ban_next`, `booking_rate90`):
  a 5th caution before the club's 19th league match, or a 10th before its
  32nd, in the previous match → P(start) 0 for the next; a served ban is
  not applied again. Probabilistic (t+1): a player one caution short
  gets P(start)_{t+1} × (1 − min(1, rate90 × P(start))), rate90 shrunk
  toward the position mean by m/(m + 4).
- **overrides.csv** (`load_overrides`; live only): rows player, gw,
  p_start, reason, timestamp_utc; an applied override is appended to
  `tools/out/minutes_overrides_log.csv` with the model's P(start) it
  replaced and scored once the gameweek settles (`--score-overrides`:
  Brier of the override and of the model against the realised start). The
  replay never reads it. The file does not exist yet (0 overrides applied).
- **Test** (`test_2526`): 2025/26 GW5–38, the recording gate as the
  population for both models (the same players each week), paired per
  gameweek; the current model = `fpl_edge.start_probability` on the S4
  harness's rebuilt inputs (`rebuild_2526`, reproduced exactly in the
  suite); co-conditions through `project_reliability` with the P(start)
  swapped in, and the keeper-starter mismatches per team-gameweek.
- **Live** (`--live`): GW8 from the export of 2026-10-18 and `fpl.sqlite`,
  the 40 largest changes with the rule that touched each.

## Limitations stated

- Cup matches are not in the data: days of rest count league kickoffs
  only, so a midweek cup tie is invisible to the congestion factor (and
  its c is fitted on the same incomplete rest).
- Replay "out" is a proxy (0 minutes after four starts): injuries flagged
  in the app before a match the player then missed are the live signal
  and cannot be rebuilt for 2025/26.
- 2025/26 cautions come from vaastav's `yellow_cards`; a season's bans
  for red cards or FA charges are not modelled.
- The co-condition (b) swaps P(start) into `project_reliability` only;
  the rest of bottomup is unchanged, so it measures the P(start) channel.
