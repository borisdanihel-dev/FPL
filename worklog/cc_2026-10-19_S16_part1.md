## What was built

`tools/blank_double.py`:
- offline, stdlib only, and run only as `python -I`, because the fixtures
  files are downloads, data never code;
- for each season with a local `fixtures.csv` (2018-19 on), it counts each
  team's fixtures in each FPL `event`. A blank is 0, a double 2 or more.
- Unscheduled fixtures are counted apart (there are none in these files).
- Events above 38 are kept as numbered and flagged.
- Teams are the season's ids; names come only from a local `teams.csv`
  (2025-26).

## Decisions for the chat

1. **2022-23 GW7.** The fixtures files have no row with event 7: the ten
   postponed matches sit in later events.
   - The ruling's fixtures-only method therefore reports GW7 as an unused
     number with no team blanks.
   - But that GW7 was a real whole-round blank: the deadline passed and
     every team scored 0. That is unlike 2019-20's numbers 30-38, which never
     existed. The files cannot tell the two apart.
   - The sign is in the totals: every other season has blanks equal to
     doubles, while 2022-23 has 22 against 42. The 20 missing blanks are GW7's.

   Keep the literal rule with this note, or count an unused number inside
   1-38 as 20 team blanks (2022-23 would become 42 / 42), with 2019-20 as an
   explicit exception? Decide before part 4 uses this history.
2. **Cosmetic:** for 2022-23 the line prints "numbered above 38: []" next to
   the unused number. The values are right.
