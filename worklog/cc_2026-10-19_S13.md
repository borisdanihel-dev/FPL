## What was built

`tools/defcon.py` (standard library only; the nightly never imports it)
and `tests/test_S13.py` (7 tests, 3 classes). Builds on S4's harness
(`canonical_at` with `hist_pit` clubs, `bootstrap_mean`), S5's
`Season2526` (the odds book, the fallback fitted on results before each
round, `lam_opp`) and `fpl_edge.reliability_inputs` (the per-90 rate, the
positional prior and the measured DEFCON reliability weight of the
training slice).

- **Thresholds used** (2025/26 rules): DEF 10 CBIT, MID 12 CBIRT, FWD 12
  CBIRT, GK none. The raw column `defensive_contribution` is CBIT for
  defenders and CBIRT for midfielders and forwards (checked on the
  2025/26 rows: every DEF row equals clearances + blocks + interceptions
  + tackles, every MID/FWD row adds recoveries). The comparator keeps
  the current code's DEF 10 / MID 12 and gives forwards 0.
- **Mean** μ = the player's shrunk per-90 rate × 90 × opponent factor;
  the rate = season actions / minutes × 90 over the training rounds,
  shrunk toward the positional median (180+ minutes) by the training
  slice's reliability weight for DEFCON; the opponent factor = λ_opp of
  the fixture / the mean λ over the round's fixtures (S5's odds-implied
  λ, the fallback labelled; no 2025/26 fixture needed it).
- **Dispersion** r per position, fitted at every origin by the method of
  moments on the model's own means over the training player-matches
  (60+ minutes, rounds < t): r = Σμ² / (Σ(x − μ)² − Σμ), clipped to
  [0.5, 1000], the Poisson limit when the variance is at or below the
  mean. **P(DEFCON)** = P(X ≥ threshold) under NB(μ, r); expected points
  2 × P.
- **Comparator**: the player's observed hit rate over his 60+-minute
  matches in the training rounds (hits / matches, no prior; 0 for
  forwards and for a player without such a match), the rule of
  `fpl_edge.defcon_expectation`, reproduced exactly on a toy export.
- **Sample**: player-matches with 60+ minutes; DEF and MID paired per
  gameweek (the primary sample), forwards shown separately. The club of
  the origin must be the row's club (a disagreement raises; none on the
  real data).
- **Test** (`--test`): 2025/26 GW5–38, Brier per gameweek comparator −
  v2, bootstrap over gameweeks (B = 10,000, 90%, seed 20261018), the
  pooled decile calibration (equal-count deciles of predicted P, the
  predicted rate of a decile = its mean P), log loss (clipped at 10⁻⁶),
  r per position per origin and the fallback share as information.

**Pre-registered result: FAIL** (both must hold). v2 is worse than the
hit-rate comparator in 26 of 34 gameweeks (better in GW5–7, 9, 12, 19,
22 and 23; from −0.056 to +0.032). The calibration table reads, without
inference: v2's mean predicted P is 0.271 against an observed hit rate of
0.229, and the gap widens with P (top decile: predicted 0.754, observed
0.427; second: 0.559 against 0.383); the bottom three deciles
under-predict (0.007–0.067 against 0.071–0.137). The comparator's deciles
are within 5 points in 5 of 10. Log loss favours v2 (0.571 against 0.890),
because the comparator's 0s and 1s are clipped at 10⁻⁶ and punished when
wrong; Brier, the pre-registered metric, does not. Fitted r: DEF 17.9 at
GW5 settling to 8.4–8.9 from GW19, MID 12.9 → 8.1–8.6, FWD 7.1–10.3; never
Poisson, never at a clip. Forwards (694 matches, hit rate 0.013): the
comparator's 0 scores Brier 0.0130, v2 0.0202. Fallback share 0.00 at
every origin. Numbers are as printed on the first full run and are not
revisited.

Candidate causes, stated as the pinned definitions that produce them,
not as fixes (nothing is tuned on this result): μ multiplies the per-90
rate by 90 on every 60+-minute match, so a player substituted after 60–89
minutes is forecast as if he played 90 (over-prediction that grows with
the rate); the dispersion is fitted on means that use the same training
rows (in-sample), so the fitted r is large and the tails extreme; the
comparator's own rule carries no prior and sits at 0 for many low-decile
players, which the Brier score rewards when they miss.

## Limitations stated

- One season; the DEFCON category exists only since 2025/26, so no
  earlier season can extend the test.
- The 60+-minute sample separates DEFCON from minutes as the ruling
  asks; live use would multiply by P(start) and the 60-minute share,
  which this test does not measure.
- The opponent factor is the odds-implied λ of the opponent; a defender's
  actions also depend on his own side's possession, which no pinned
  input carries.
