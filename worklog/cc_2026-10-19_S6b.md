- **own_proxy** = `fpl_edge.project_reliability` on
  `reliability_inputs(canonical_through(canonical_at(hist, t), t − 1))`,
  the harness's `bottomup`, the only 2025/26-rebuildable source (S7 and
  S8 replayed it). What it lacks against live `own` (`fpl_edge.project`),
  stated in the output: the form term (points over the last four
  gameweeks per 90, shrunk toward the positional or personal prior), the
  minutes-reliability scaling, the pre-deadline FDR adjustment
  (1 + (3 − FDR) × 0.12), and `chance_next_round` and `status`. own is a
  form rate; own_proxy a bottom-up expectation.
- **xp_v2** = P(start) × (E_xg + E_bl) / 2 + P(sub) × E[pts | sub], S6's
  equal blend as frozen: P(start) the current minutes model; E_bl | start
  = S5's `bottomup_lambda` / P(start); E_xg | start = `project_xg`'s
  terms conditional on starting with S11's allocation for the attacking
  rates (goals = xG | start × GOAL_VALUE, assists = xA | start × 3, where
  xG | start = Σ λ_team × shrunk share × mean minutes per start / 90),
  S5's odds-implied clean sheet × CS_VALUE, appearance 2, DEFCON 2 × the
  hit rate over 60+-minute training matches, bonus 0.8 × the ICT
  percentile; P(sub) over the last min(4, t − 1) rounds and E[pts | sub]
  per position from the official points of sub appearances before t (what
  the model saw). The recording gate (45+ minutes in the last four)
  applies to both models.
- **The test:** S7's 50 squads (seed 7), fixed, no transfers, no chips;
  every gameweek each model picks the XI (`chips.xi_and_bench`) and the
  captain (the highest projection in the XI) and the realised points use
  `rule_autosubs` on the re-scored minutes with the captain doubled; per
  gameweek the mean over squads of (xp_v2 − own_proxy); bootstrap over
  gameweeks (B = 10,000, 90%, seed 20261018); pass: lower bound > 0.
  Secondary, printed only: per-gameweek Spearman and MAE among predicted
  starters, paired; the share of squad-gameweeks with the same XI and
  captain.

## Limitations stated

- One season, one set of 50 synthetic squads; the squads are fixed, so
  the test measures XI and captain choice, not transfers.
- own_proxy is not own; the gap to live own is stated, not measured.
- The 2026/27 re-scoring keeps the official 2025/26 bonus (Step 10's
  rule) and uses the live `game_config` as the points table.
- The xg half's clean sheet uses S5's odds-implied probability where live
  `project_xg` uses the FDR table (not rebuildable); stated.
