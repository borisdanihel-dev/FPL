## What was built

`tools/odds_lambda.py` (standard library only; the nightly never imports
it — tested; the live Odds API collector is lane A's W1 and `ODDS_API_KEY`
never appears here) and `tests/test_S5.py` (10 tests). Data:
`tools/odds/E0_2025-26_fetched_2026-10-18.csv` (380 rows) and
`E0_2026-27_fetched_2026-10-18.csv` (60 rows, to 12 Oct), downloaded with
Boris's approval (amendment 1 point 2), committed with their fetch date.

- **Odds**: `AvgH/D/A`, `Avg>2.5/<2.5`; a row lacking them falls back to
  `B365*` (none did); closing (`*C*`) and Pinnacle columns are never read.
  Team names map through five aliases (Man United, Tottenham, Coventry,
  Hull, Ipswich) to the season's team table; an unmapped name stops the
  run. Rows join fixtures on (date, home, away) with a one-day tolerance.
- **De-margin**: proportional and power (k by bisection); the forecast
  uses the power method, the proportional variant is a secondary.
- **λ**: total from P(under 2.5) by bisection on the Poisson CDF at 2;
  home/away split by bisection on P(home win) under independent Poisson
  (to 15 goals); P(clean sheet) = exp(−λ_opp).
- **Fallback**: a time-decayed Poisson team model (weights
  exp(−0.0065 × days before the deadline), alternating attack/defence
  updates, home factor, league average under 4 weighted matches), trained
  on results before the deadline; the source that fired is printed per
  fixture. It fired 0 times in 2025/26 (every fixture had odds).
- **Source `bottomup_lambda`**: bottomup with the GK/DEF clean-sheet term's
  λ replaced by λ_opp and a goals-conceded term −P(start) × E[floor(GC/2)]
  under Poisson(λ_opp); MID and FWD unchanged. Unit test: a stronger
  opponent's odds give a lower P(clean sheet), everything else equal.
- **2025/26 backtest**: GW5–38, the S4 harness (`canonical_at`, clubs of the
  origin; training rounds < g); the fallback's deadline is the round's
  first kickoff (2025/26 has no recorded deadlines). Sample: GK/DEF with 60+
  minutes in the fixture (the clean-sheet eligibility rule on the outcome).
- **2026/27 condition**: for each settled gameweek the newest export
  generated before its deadline rebuilds `bottomup` exactly as
  `record_status` does (checked against the recorded log rows: max
  difference 0.000) and `bottomup_lambda` from the same inputs plus the
  2026/27 odds; starters = rebuilt P(start) ≥ 0.8; DEF/GK Spearman per
  gameweek.
