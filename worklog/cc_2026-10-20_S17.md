## What was run

- **The leagues:** 200 synthetic leagues of 10 from the 2025/26 replay.
  - S7's sampler with style weights: 4 template (∝ selected), 3 mixed
    (∝ selected^0.5) and 3 differential (∝ 1/selected).
  - Fixed squads, no chips.
- **The truth:** GW2–38. Each manager's XI and captain come from S6b's
  point-in-time xp_v2(u), with `rule_autosubs`, the captain doubled, and
  Step 10's re-score.
- **The simulator from GW20, 26 and 32:**
  - the base is the truth over GW2..T−1;
  - XI and captain come from the origin's xp_v2 matrix (week T on S5's
    lambdas, later weeks on the origin's fallback);
  - one draw per player-week from Step 14's PMFs, shared by every owner,
    with the captain's draw counted twice;
  - 2,000 simulations, seed 20261018.
- **Metric:** Brier skill against "the current leader wins" (ties split),
  pooled. The bar is skill ≥ 0.05, plus the deciles.

- **Limitations (stated):**
  - no autosubs inside the simulation;
  - players outside the origin's matrix draw 0 there, but score in the
