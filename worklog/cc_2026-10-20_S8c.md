## What was built

- **`tools/planner_live.py`:**
  - the matrix is S15's `builder.live_matrix`, unchanged (xp_v2 as tested,
    target..target+5). Each fixture's lambda is from the Odds API snapshot
    where it prices it, else the fallback. Every fixture's source is
    printed per week.
  - S8's `rank_plans`, unchanged: k = 1 free transfer,
    `hits_allowed=False`, decay 0.85, schedule `boris`, S8's market and
    pool. The top 5 plans are printed.
  - the simple rule beside it: S8's `baseline_plan` on week t's matrix.
  - the GW8 Bench Boost variant (information): every scored plan with
    week t re-scored as all 15 + the captain, week t's gain replaced
    only, re-ranked, the top 3.
  - no hit is ever suggested; the line says hits are a manual call.
- **`tools/gw_pass.py` section 3:**
  - prints this planner as the default and the simple rule beside it,
    then S8's own planner as information.
  - On a missing input or failure, it says so and prints the ruling's
    fallback (S8's planner and the simple rule, decided by hand). Nothing
    is substituted.
  - The bundle's first line and the section title name the ruling. The
    other sections are unchanged.
- **`tools/builder.py` (S15 amendment 2):** `live_rule` runs S15's
  `rule_candidates` on the live export with the live matrix, then the
  frozen `fpl_edge.build_three_layer_squad`, unchanged.
  - The budget is the live budget (bank + selling prices), at current
    prices.
  - The print shows the rule's squad, its spend, its objective on the
    builder's objective, and builder − rule.
  - The rule's squad is a start only when it is legal with the cap on.
  - A short squad is reported, and nothing is substituted.
  - The header names the acceptance for `--wildcard` and `--freehit`;
    `--bb` stays information.
