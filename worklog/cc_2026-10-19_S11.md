## What was built

`tools/shares.py` (standard library only; the nightly never imports it)
and `tests/test_S11.py` (8 tests, 3 classes), as pinned:

- Team xG of a fixture = Σ `expected_goals` of the club's rows in it; the
  player's current share s = own / team over the fixtures he appeared in
  (training rounds); 0 without an appearance. xA likewise.
- Prior s0 = his 2024/25 share at the same club (whole season), else the
  2024/25 club-position mean (players with 180+ minutes there), else the
  league-position mean (clubs without 2024/25 rows), labelled.
- Credibility Z = m / (m + k), m = minutes / 90 in the training rounds;
  k = n (1 − r) / r from the split-half reliability of the share on
  2024/25 (odd against even rounds, players with 45+ minutes in each
  half, n = their mean minutes / 90 per half), measured once.
- Expected minutes share = P(start) (the recording model's) × the player's
  mean minutes per start / 90 (the position mean without a start).
- Forecast xG = Σ over the club's round-t fixtures of λ_team × (Z s +
  (1 − Z) s0) × minutes share; xA the same with the xA shares; goals
  forecast = xG, assists forecast = xA (finishing shrunk to 0).
- Comparator = the same with Z = 1 (the current-season share alone).
- "manager changes: not modelled (no dated source in the repo)" is
  printed (ruling 2).
- Test: GW5–38, predicted starters (P(start) ≥ 0.8) with a fixture, the
  per-gameweek tie-corrected Spearman of forecast against realised goals
  and assists, v2 − comparator, bootstrap over gameweeks (B = 10,000, seed
  20261018), Holm across the two at 90% (the smaller p at 95%, then the
  other at 90%, stopping at the first failure); each metric adopted
  separately only with its Holm-adjusted lower bound > 0.

[+0.0018, +0.0123] (0.249 → 0.243). v2 ranks goals better in 21 of 34
gameweeks (worse in 10, equal in 3) and assists in 20 (worse in 12). The
mean Z rises from 0.55 at GW5 to 0.89 at GW38, so the prior's weight fades
as the season's minutes accrue; the prior came from the player's own
2024/25 share for 58% of the sampled player-weeks (85 of 133 at GW5, 56
of 89 at GW38), the club-position mean for the rest with 180+ minutes, and
the league-position mean for 6–29 a week (the promoted clubs and the
unmatched). The improvements are small in absolute terms (0.006 and 0.004
of rank correlation) and the assists interval sits just above zero;
these are the numbers as pre-registered and are not revisited.

## Limitations stated

- Name matching is exact on vaastav's `name`; a renamed player falls to
  the club-position mean (counted and printed per week).
- One prior season; promoted clubs have none, so their players start from
  the league-position mean.
- The minutes share uses the recording model's P(start); S12's v2 is not
  adopted and not used.
- The test ranks players within a gameweek; it says nothing about the
  level of xG, which the comparator shares.
