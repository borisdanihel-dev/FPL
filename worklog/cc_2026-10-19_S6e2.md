## What was run

- **The model.** For goalkeepers: xp_v2 as tested + P(start) × Σ over the
  round's fixtures of E[floor(S_f/3)], with S_f ~ Poisson(μ_f).
  - μ_f is S6e's E[saves | start] for that fixture alone (S6e's functions
    unchanged).
  - A double gameweek sums two floors.
  - k uses S6e's split-half rule on the prior season's keepers, here
    2023/24: k = 17.40, r 0.371, 37 keepers.
- **The test.** S6e's `test_2526` loop runs unchanged with these
  forecasts, on S6f's 2024/25 harness (outcomes from S10b's re-score with
  DEFCON excluded). Only 2024/25 is used; S6e saw 2025/26.
