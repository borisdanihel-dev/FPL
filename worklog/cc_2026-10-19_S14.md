## What was built

`tools/points_pmf.py`. Per player-gameweek, the PMF is P(start) × (½ X +
½ L) + P(sub) × U + (1 − P(start) − P(sub)) × 0:

- **X, the xg half given a start:**
  - goals and assists Poisson at S11's per-fixture xG and xA;
  - clean sheet Bernoulli at exp(−λ_opp), from S5;
  - DEFCON Bernoulli at the hit rate;
  - appearance, DEFCON and bonus once per gameweek, as xp_v2 counts them;
  - bonus from the empirical table of starts in rounds < t by returns cell
    (group × goal involvements × clean sheet, 30-row back-off), tilted to
    0.8 × the ICT percentile.
- **L, the bottomup half given a start**, per fixture:
  - `project_reliability`'s xGI split 0.62 / 0.38;
  - GK/DEF clean sheet and goals conceded from one Poisson(λ_opp) count;
  - MID's own team-rate clean sheet;
  - logistic DEFCON.
- **U:** the empirical points of sub appearances per position.
- **No saves term.**
- Independence as stated in the prereg. Where P(start) + P(sub) > 1 (49
  player-gameweeks in 2025/26, around doubles), the two parts are added as
  independent events so the mean still matches.
