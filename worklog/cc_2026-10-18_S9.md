## What was built

`tools/league.py` (standard library only; the nightly never imports it;
the LEAGUE ODDS section and `fetched_at` on the league tables are lane
A's W2) and `tests/test_S9.py` (5 tests). Reads `entries`, `entry_gw`,
`picks`, `events` and the projection log (`own`, S2 reader rule) from the
data worktree, read-only; writes `tools/out/league_<date>.txt` only.

- **Points** = gameweek points net of hits (`entry_gw.points − transfers_cost`;
  `points` is the gross score, equal to Σ pick multiplier × player points
  on GW5 for every manager), settled gameweeks only (lockdown).
- **σ_i, σ_d,j, ρ_ij** per rival; **pooled σ_d** = root mean square of
  σ_d,j (primary); σ_d without chip weeks (a gameweek where either side had
  a chip active is dropped from that pair) as information; 90% CIs by
  bootstrap over gameweeks (B = 10,000, seed 20261018, the statistic
  recomputed per resample).
- **P(we finish above j)** = Φ(−gap_j / (σ_d √n)), gap_j = j's total −
  ours, n = 38 − last settled gameweek, zero drift (primary); the variance
  line gap_j > σ_d √n ("beyond the line" / "inside"; a gap exactly on the
  line is inside); the drift version (information) from each side's last
  settled XI valued by the coming gameweek's `own` rows, applied over
  min(n, 6) gameweeks.
- **Threat set** = rivals ahead; if we lead, rivals with P(j above us) ≥
  0.05; weights w_j = P(j above us), normalised.
- **League EO** per player = Σ w_j × multiplier_j from each threat rival's
  last settled picks (the coming gameweek's picks are hidden until its
  deadline, and the output says so); multipliers starter 1, captain 2,
  triple captain 3, bench 0, bench 1 under Bench Boost — the picks'
  multiplier field, cross-checked against position / is_captain /
  `active_chip` on every settled gameweek (a mismatch stops the run).
- **Relative xP** = (our multiplier − EO) × `own` for the coming gameweek,
  our 15 being our last settled picks (the database holds no pre-deadline
  picks); "free variance" when an option within 0.3 xP of the top has a
  lower threat-set EO.
- **Captain table**: our XI by `own` (any formation, any position), the
  differential allowance ≤ 0.5 xP before GW25 and ≤ 1.0 from GW25 against
  the threat-set EO, and the running count over settled gameweeks (each
  with its own pre-deadline `own` rows and our picks) of how often a DEF or
  GK topped it.
- The "next gameweek" is the coming deadline (GW8 on 2026-10-18; GW7 is
  played but not settled), while gaps, n and the variance line rest on
  settled gameweeks only; the header says both.
