## What was run

- **Dates:** every gameweek 5–38 of 2025/26 and of 2024/25, each a
  hypothetical wildcard at that gameweek's prices with 100.0. 68 dates.
- **One xp_v2 matrix for both sides** (S8b's horizon season): week t on
  S5's lambdas (S6b's), weeks t+1..t+5 on the origin's fallback.
- **The builder:**
  - the decayed six-week objective (0.85), the best XI in any formation,
    the captain, S7's bench weights;
  - greedy + local search over S8's pool (singles best improvement, pairs
    first improvement), also started from the rule's squad;
  - the GK+DEF 2-per-club cap off in the A/B, its cost reported.
- **The rule:**
  - `fpl_edge.build_three_layer_squad` unchanged, 4-3-3, the
    quota-proportional split;
  - its candidates ported to history (layer 1, layer 3 with the GK/DEF
    six-week sum = the shared matrix);
  - the port equals `fpl_edge.wildcard_candidates` exactly on the real
    GW7 export (every field, ranks, exclusions and the squad).
- **Realised:** weeks t..t+5 by the origin's matrix, `rule_autosubs`, the
  captain the highest xp_v2. Outcomes are Step 10's 2025/26 and S10b's
  2024/25 re-scores.
- **A crash and a fix, before any result:** the first run stopped at
  2024/25 GW23 on assistant-manager rows (position "AM", in 2024/25 from
  GW23; KeyError in the greedy fill). In `91f401d` they leave the market,
  with a test, and the whole A/B was rerun. 2025/26 has no such rows.
