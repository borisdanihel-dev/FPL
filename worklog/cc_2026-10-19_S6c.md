## own_rebuilt, exactly

`fpl_edge.project` called as `record_status` calls it for source own, per
fixture of round t, summed over a double, rounded to 3 decimals as the log
records it, kept when > 0; gate `mins_last4` ≥ 45, status taken as `a`.

- form term: official 2025/26 points and minutes over rounds t−4..t−1 (the
  export's `event BETWEEN gw−3 AND gw`, gw = t − 1; equal to the harness's
  `mins_last4`, asserted in the tests); `gws_played` = t − 1;
- positional prior: `positional_priors` over every harness player at t;
- personal prior: `personal_priors` on 2024/25 totals per full name held by
  one 2024/25 element, ≥ 450 minutes, keyed by the 2025/26 `web_name`;
- FDR: `fixtures.csv` `team_h_difficulty` (home) / `team_a_difficulty`
  (away), end of season.

What it lacks against live own: the pre-deadline FDR; status and
`chance_next_round` (taken as available); personal priors before 2024/25;
FPL-id identity for the personal prior (full name; an ambiguous name gets
none). Printed in the output.
