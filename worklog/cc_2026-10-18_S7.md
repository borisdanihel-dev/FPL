## What was built

`tools/chips.py` (standard library only; the nightly never imports it —
tested) and `tests/test_S7.py` (13 tests). Builds on S4's harness
(`tools/backtest.py`, `tools/hist_pit.py`) and on `fpl_edge`'s frozen
pieces: `project()` (the `own` formula), `_best_xi`, `best_one_week_xi` (the
item N Free Hit solver), `free_hit_table` / `free_hit_lines` (the item N
screen), `rule_autosubs`, `selling_budget`, `selling_price`.

- **xP source `own`** (ruling 1): for the next gameweek the recorded row
  (S2 reader rule); for the later fixture-known gameweeks `project()` on
  that week's fixture(s) — the formula `record_status` uses, through the
  same gate (status `a`, 45+ minutes in the last four); a blank or a
  gated-out player scores 0. Weeks beyond the export's six are "proxy"
  weeks carrying the last fixture-known week's values; the table says so.
- **V_BB,t** = Σ bench xP × (1 − w), w = 0.30 / 0.10 / 0.03 outfield in
  forecast order, 0.03 sub keeper (ruling 2): the bench points a Bench
  Boost adds beyond the automatic substitutions that happen anyway.
  Clarification of a wording slip in the prereg's acceptance test 1 ("in
  the planned BB week w = 1.0 … so V_BB there is the full bench xP"): the
  ruling's "1.0 in a planned Bench Boost week" is the weight the bench
  carries in the team's expected points that week (used by bench prep and
  the team total); the chip's *value* is the gain over a normal week, Σ xP
  (1 − w), everywhere. The replay's realised BB gain is defined the same
  way (bench points minus the autosubs that would have come on).
- **V_FH,t** = best legal one-week XI under the selling budget (item N
  solver) minus our best XI from the current 15 by the same xP; every
  Free Hit value is labelled "no transfers assumed" (amendment 1 point 4).
- **Induction** (ruling 3): V(t, S) over t = next .. 19 and S ⊆ {BB, FH},
  one chip a week, FH never in GW19, V(20, ·) = 0. A tie goes to playing
  (the chips are worth 0 after GW19, so holding at equal value only risks
  losing one), BB before FH on a tie between them. Output: the V table per
  week, the continuation values for every chip set, PLAY BB / PLAY FH /
  HOLD for the next gameweek with its margin over the best other action.
- **Uncertainty** (ruling 2): residuals (realised − `own`) by position from
  every 2026/27 gameweek the S4 harness has graded; 1,000 resamples
  perturb each player of our 15 and of the FH XI by a residual of his
  position, with the XIs held fixed (the solver is not re-run per
  resample, a stated simplification); printed: the 90% interval of the
  recommended action's margin and the share of resamples with the same
  action.
- **Bench prep**: for the planned BB week, per bench slot the best
  same-position replacement by that week's xP within bank + selling
  price, club cap kept (information only).
- **Free Hit screen** (ruling 5): item N's table and flags, which already
  use the selling budget (S2); printed by the live run.
- **Replay** (ruling 4): 2025/26 GW5–19 on 50 synthetic squads sampled
  from GW1 ownership (seed 7; weighted draws without replacement by
  `selected`, quotas 2/5/5/3, club cap 3, £100.0m at GW1 `value`,
  rejection on cost), squads held fixed, prices of the origin round,
  selling prices by the FPL rule, clubs as of the origin (`hist_pit`).
  Forecasts at origin t for weeks u ≥ t: `project_reliability` trained on
  rounds < t (`canonical_through(canonical_at(hist, t), t − 1)`) on week
  u's fixture pairings — the only source rebuildable for 2025/26 under S4
  ruling 2 (`own` is not), used alike for every comparator. Policies:
  calculator (the induction at each t with the chips left), heuristic
  (BB when the forecast bench xP at t is the highest of the remaining
  weeks; FH when the forecast gap at t is, t ≠ 19), both-early (BB GW5,
  FH GW6). Realised chip points: BB = bench points minus the autosubs
  that would have come on (`rule_autosubs` on realised minutes, bench in
  forecast order); FH = realised FH XI (captain = highest forecast,
  doubled) minus realised own XI. Paired bootstrap over the 50 squads
  (B = 10,000, 90%, seed 20261018).

## Limitations stated

- `own` is nearly flat across players and weeks, so the chip values are
  close and the recommendation is fragile; the resampling share says so.
- Proxy weeks beyond the export's fixtures carry the last known week's
  values; the induction cannot see fixture swings after GW13 until the
  export does.
- V_FH assumes no transfers (our 15 held fixed) — an upper bound until
  Step 8.
- The replay's 50 squads share one season (common shocks are not resampled
  away), use `bottomup` not `own`, and hold squads fixed for fifteen weeks.
- The replay heuristic and the calculator re-plan every week on
  point-in-time forecasts; a chip can be chased later by both.
